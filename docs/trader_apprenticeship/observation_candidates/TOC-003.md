# TRADER_OBSERVATION_CANDIDATE_TOC-003

- **CANDIDATE_ID**: TOC-003
- **ORIGIN**: AI_TRADER_MARKET_APPRENTICESHIP
- **DISCOVERY_DATE**: 2020-04-16 to 2020-04-22 (in-replay); frozen 2026-08-25 (real session date)
- **DISCOVERY_QUARTER**: 2020-Q2
- **KNOWLEDGE_VERSION**: post-Q1-checkpoint, pre-Q2-checkpoint (Q2 walk still in progress)

- **FROZEN_OBSERVATION_DEFINITION**: When a price level has been defended 3+ times by real trading
  volume (not thin/overnight volume) before finally breaking, the reliability of that break is NOT
  determined by the break itself — it is determined by whether the first 1-2 M15 bars immediately
  following the break show genuine continuation (no meaningful rejection wick against the new
  direction, no stall) versus a stall or whipsaw back toward the broken level. Breaks that continue
  immediately have meaningfully better odds than breaks that stall first.

- **MARKET_CONTEXT**: XAUUSD, 2020-04-16 through 2020-04-22 (in-replay), a single continuous
  extended countertrend rally against the unchanged BEARISH H4 structural context (price +100-150pts
  off the quarter low over this stretch). All 6 instances occurred within this one regime/leg —
  generalization outside it is untested.

- **STRUCTURAL_LOGIC**: A working hypothesis, not a proven mechanism: a level that has already
  absorbed 3+ real-volume attempts has been actively fought over by two-sided participants. When it
  finally breaks, the break itself could reflect either (a) genuine exhaustion of the defending side
  and real follow-through demand/supply, or (b) a temporary imbalance (a single large order, a
  liquidity sweep) that lacks sustained participation behind it. The first 1-2 bars after the break
  are the most direct observable evidence distinguishing these two cases: immediate continuation
  implies (a), an immediate stall implies (b).

- **WHAT_THE_TRADER_BELIEVES_IS_HAPPENING**: Heavily-defended levels are genuine battlegrounds, not
  arbitrary lines. The break event alone carries little information about which side actually won the
  battle — the market reveals that in the bars immediately following, not in the break bar itself.

- **WHY_IT_MAY_MATTER**: This directly refines TRADER_LESSON_021 (the general caution that
  heavily-defended-level breaks are not extra-reliable) into something closer to actionable: it is not
  that these breaks are uniformly unreliable, but that they are a MIXED population that can be
  meaningfully split into two sub-populations using only 1-2 bars of additional, already-available
  evidence — without needing to skip every such setup outright.

- **COUNTEREXAMPLE_TIMESTAMPS**: none yet — the split has been clean across all 6 instances so far
  (both "immediate continuation" instances won, all 4 "stalled first" instances lost). Watch for a
  future instance that breaks this clean split (e.g., immediate continuation that still reverses, or
  a stall that still works out) — that would be the first genuine counterexample.

- **TIMEFRAMES_INVOLVED**: M15 only — all 6 instances observed and traded at M15 resolution. Whether
  this generalizes to H1/H4 breakout-continuation reads is untested.

- **EXPECTED_MARKET_BEHAVIOR**: A break of a level with 3+ prior real-volume defenses should be judged
  by its first 1-2 bars, not entered/held purely on the break itself; immediate continuation (no
  rejection) meaningfully favors the trade, an immediate stall meaningfully disfavors it.

- **POTENTIAL_INVALIDATION**: A future instance where immediate continuation still reverses hard, or
  where a stalled break still ultimately works, would weaken this candidate. A regime where levels are
  not fought over with real volume (calmer markets) would make this candidate's precondition
  (3+ real-volume defenses) rare and largely untestable there.
