# TRADER_OBSERVATION_CANDIDATE_TOC-002

- **CANDIDATE_ID**: TOC-002
- **ORIGIN**: AI_TRADER_MARKET_APPRENTICESHIP
- **DISCOVERY_DATE**: 2020-03-10 to 2020-03-12 (in-replay); frozen 2026-08-25 (real session date)
- **DISCOVERY_QUARTER**: 2020-Q1
- **KNOWLEDGE_VERSION**: pre-checkpoint (2020-Q1 checkpoint not yet frozen; this candidate observed
  during the walk that will inform it)

- **FROZEN_OBSERVATION_DEFINITION**: During a high-volatility, high-participation multi-day stretch, a
  multi-bar M15 hold (5-6 consecutive closes on one side of a contested price level) is NOT by itself a
  reliable signal that the level has been genuinely resolved. Such holds can still fail and reverse,
  even when they clear the "wait for multi-bar confirmation" bar that successfully filtered out earlier
  single-bar/2-bar fakeouts at the same or similar levels.

- **MARKET_CONTEXT**: XAUUSD, 2020-03-10 through 2020-03-12 (in-replay), a single continuous
  high-volatility stretch (COVID-19/oil-price-war-era panic) featuring repeated fast expansion moves,
  deep wicks, and heavy-volume whipsaws at specific price levels (1657, then 1641.7) inside the broader
  1621-1703 multi-week range. All 3 instances occurred within this one stretch — generalization to calmer
  regimes is untested.

- **STRUCTURAL_LOGIC**: A working hypothesis, not a proven mechanism: in a regime with unusually high
  two-sided participation, a price level can attract enough sustained opposing interest that even 5-6
  bars of apparent acceptance still represents contested, not settled, territory. Ordinary support/
  resistance logic (multi-bar hold = confirmation) may implicitly assume calmer, more one-sided
  liquidity conditions than this stretch provided.

- **WHAT_THE_TRADER_BELIEVES_IS_HAPPENING**: During unusually volatile stretches, specific price levels
  can become genuine two-sided battlegrounds where volume flows into both breaking and defending the
  level repeatedly — bar-count alone doesn't distinguish "real acceptance" from "the contest is still
  ongoing."

- **WHY_IT_MAY_MATTER**: This directly qualifies the practical fix I adopted after TOC-001-style single-
  bar fakeouts (that is, "wait for a multi-bar hold before trusting direction"). That fix is necessary but
  evidently not sufficient in this kind of regime — some additional confirmation (price reaching a fresh
  reference beyond the contested level, or a visible drop-off in counter-volume) may be needed before a
  multi-bar hold can be trusted here.

- **EXAMPLE_TIMESTAMPS**:
  1. M15 bar close ~1583858700 (2020-03-10 ~17:20 UTC) — 5 consecutive closes below 1657 (1654.2, 1656.2,
     1653.0, 1654.2) called CONFIRMED SHORT; reclaimed back above 1657 within the next 3 bars without ever
     reaching the stated 1649.5 target.
  2. M15 bar close ~1583973900 (2020-03-11 ~23:15 UTC) — 6 consecutive closes above 1641.7 (1644.0, 1644.3,
     1644.9, 1647.4) called a genuine multi-bar reclaim; broke back below 1641.7 with real volume
     (2717-2937) within the next 3 bars, extending to a fresh low (1631.0).
  3. M15 bar close ~1584006300 (2020-03-12 ~08:15 UTC) — 5 consecutive closes at/above 1641.7 (1643.1,
     1643.5, 1642.1, 1643.0, 1644.1) called a second reclaim attempt; failed again within 2 bars, dropping
     back toward 1631.0.

- **COUNTEREXAMPLE_TIMESTAMPS**: none yet identified — every multi-bar hold attempted during this specific
  stretch has failed. This is itself notable and should be watched: if a future multi-bar hold in this
  same stretch DOES hold, that would be the first counterexample and worth logging explicitly (not
  silently treated as "expected").

- **TIMEFRAMES_INVOLVED**: M15 only so far — all 3 instances were observed and called at M15 resolution.
  Whether this generalizes to H1/H4 multi-bar holds is untested (H4 context has remained RANGE throughout,
  unaffected by any of these M15-level whipsaws).

- **EXPECTED_MARKET_BEHAVIOR**: In a high-volatility, high-participation regime, do not treat a 5-6 bar
  M15 hold at a contested level as confirmed direction; require either continuation to a fresh reference
  beyond the level, or a visible volume drop-off, before trusting it.

- **POTENTIAL_INVALIDATION**: A future multi-bar hold in this same volatility regime that DOES hold and
  extend cleanly would be a genuine counterexample, weakening this candidate. A calmer regime where
  multi-bar holds behave normally (as expected support/resistance) would suggest this candidate is
  regime-specific rather than a general market-reading rule — worth testing explicitly once volatility
  normalizes later in the quarter.

- **RELATION_TO_TOC-001**: A more specific, higher-bar-count version of the same underlying caution
  TOC-001 established (fresh extremes fading rather than holding) — TOC-001 was about single-bar/
  1-2-bar fades; TOC-002 shows the same unreliability persists even after raising the confirmation bar
  to 5-6 consecutive closes, within this particular volatility regime.
