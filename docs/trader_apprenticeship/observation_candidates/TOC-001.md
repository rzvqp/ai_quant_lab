# TRADER_OBSERVATION_CANDIDATE_TOC-001

- **CANDIDATE_ID**: TOC-001
- **ORIGIN**: AI_TRADER_MARKET_APPRENTICESHIP
- **DISCOVERY_DATE**: 2020-02-19 (in-replay date); frozen 2026-08-24 (real session date)
- **DISCOVERY_QUARTER**: 2020-Q1
- **KNOWLEDGE_VERSION**: pre-checkpoint (2020-Q1 checkpoint not yet frozen; this candidate observed
  during the walk that will inform it)

- **FROZEN_OBSERVATION_DEFINITION**: When XAUUSD (H4 or M15) prints a fresh multi-week price extreme
  that meaningfully exceeds the prior tested high of an established range, the extreme is not held —
  price gives back most or all of the new extension within 1-2 bars of the same timeframe that printed
  it, rather than continuing or consolidating at the new level. This held true even for an extension
  that briefly held for 2 M15 bars before failing (the most "convincing" instance observed).

- **MARKET_CONTEXT**: XAUUSD, 2020-01-01 through 2020-02-19 (in-replay), a range-bound regime bounded
  roughly 1517-1593 since a Jan 1-2 breakout, itself following a 2019 uptrend. All 4 instances occurred
  within this single multi-week regime — generalization outside it is untested.

- **STRUCTURAL_LOGIC**: A working hypothesis, not a proven mechanism: fresh extremes in an already-wide,
  well-tested range may attract short-term momentum/stop-run participation that lacks the follow-through
  liquidity to sustain a genuine breakout, so the extension gets faded once that initial flow exhausts.
  This is a description of what was observed, not a causal claim.

- **WHAT_THE_TRADER_BELIEVES_IS_HAPPENING**: The market is repeatedly "probing" the edges of its own
  established range without yet being ready to leave it — each probe attracts enough opposing flow to
  reverse it, at least within this specific multi-week window.

- **WHY_IT_MAY_MATTER**: If durable, this would argue AGAINST chasing a fresh breakout print in this kind
  of regime, and FOR waiting for genuine multi-bar acceptance (not just 1-2 bars) before trusting a new
  extreme — directly actionable for entry-timing discipline, consistent with the mandate's own emphasis
  on not chasing an already-spent move.

- **EXAMPLE_TIMESTAMPS**:
  1. H4 bar close 1578031199 (2020-01-03 ~04:00 UTC) — spike to 1589.7, H1 detail shows partial rejection
     within 1-2 H1 bars.
  2. H4 bar close 1578463199 (2020-01-08 ~02:00 UTC) — spike to 1611.5, next H1 bar gave back ~40%.
  3. H4 bar close ~1580507999 (2020-02-03 ~10:00 UTC) — fresh high 1592.168, fully round-tripped by the
     same H1 bar's own close.
  4. M15 bar close 1582035300-1582036200 (2020-02-19 ~03:15-04:15 UTC) — fresh high 1593.414, held for 2
     M15 bars (close 1592.776, 1592.43) before a sharp reversal (low 1588.089, close 1589.068, heaviest
     volume of the entire walk).

- **COUNTEREXAMPLE_TIMESTAMPS**: **CONFIRMED** — the breakout beginning M15 bar close 1582038900
  (2020-02-19 ~06:15 UTC, fresh high 1598.368) has, as of bar close 1582081200 (~2020-02-20 06:45 UTC),
  held for 44 consecutive M15 candles (~11 hours) through multiple minor pullback-and-recovery cycles
  without ever reverting anywhere close to its origin — categorically different from all 4 confirming
  instances (each failed within 1-2 bars). Whether this specific move eventually reverts at some later
  point is irrelevant to the counterexample itself: the FROZEN_OBSERVATION_DEFINITION's claimed 1-2 bar
  rejection window has already been decisively violated. This does not by itself invalidate the whole
  candidate (4 confirming vs. 1 disconfirming instance is a real, mixed record, not proof either way) —
  it means the definition as originally written is too narrow/absolute and needs revision. See the
  ledger entry for the resulting scope note; STATUS below is updated accordingly.

- **TIMEFRAMES_INVOLVED**: Primarily H4 and M15; the pattern was first noticed at H4 (instances 1-3),
  then confirmed at M15 resolution (instance 4), suggesting it may not be timeframe-specific within this
  regime, but that itself has not been tested at H1 or M5.

- **EXPECTED_MARKET_BEHAVIOR**: A fresh multi-week extreme in this kind of range-bound regime is more
  likely to reverse within 1-2 bars than to hold/extend.

- **POTENTIAL_INVALIDATION**: A future fresh extreme (in this same regime or a similar one) that holds
  for 3+ bars and genuinely extends the range would directly weaken this candidate; a regime change
  (a confirmed breakout that never reverts) would invalidate its scope entirely.

- **STATUS**: UNVALIDATED_TRADER_OBSERVATION
