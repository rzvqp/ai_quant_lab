# Addendum A to AP2-DC-0002

**Date**: 2026-07-25
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0011
**Against**: AP2-DC-0002 (A Major Scheduled Political Catalyst Produces a Multi-Hour,
Near-Record-Volume, Complex Two-Way Volatility Episode Far Exceeding Any Routine Data-Release
Reaction)

## Observation

The original candidate characterized the episode as resolving by ~10:15 UTC on 2024-11-06, settling
~2723-2727 after volume normalized to 3186-3606. Continued observation past that point (this same
in-replay date, 2024-11-06) shows the episode did **not** fully resolve there -- after a brief
~1hr lull (10:15-11:15 UTC, volume back to baseline 2807-3457), a second, substantially larger wave
began:

- **11:15-12:15 UTC**: decline resumes, 2721 -> 2696.06, volume rebuilding (4219 -> 6368).
- **13:00-14:30 UTC**: the decisive acceleration -- volume surges to near-record levels multiple
  times in succession (10158 at 13:00 UTC, exceeding this candidate's original peak of 9981;
  8827, 9607, 9910, 9515, 9579 across the following candles), driving price from ~2694 down to a
  fresh episode low of **2652.40** (14:30 UTC) -- well below the original candidate's documented
  low of 2701.40.
- **14:45-16:00 UTC**: continued volatile two-way churn in the 2652-2678 range, volume still
  elevated (7398-8799) but gradually fading.
- **16:00-18:00 UTC**: volume progressively normalizes (7693 -> 5224 -> 4900 -> 3472-4221), price
  settling ~2663-2669 -- below both the original candidate's settlement (~2723-2727) and its
  original low (2701.40).

Combined, the full episode now spans **~00:15 UTC to ~18:00 UTC on 2024-11-06** -- nearly 18 hours
total (with one ~1hr lull), taking price from a pre-episode baseline of ~2740-2744 down to an
overall low of 2652.40 -- a total **~88-92pt decline** from the original baseline, roughly
double the ~48.6pt range documented in the original candidate. Peak single-candle volume across
the full combined episode is 10158 (13:00 UTC this addendum), exceeding the original candidate's
9981 peak, though still below the single largest volumes observed elsewhere this replay
(12999, 12640, 12627, 12451 -- all tied to scheduled monthly NFP releases).

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0002

This second wave shares the same underlying calendar catalyst (continued market digestion of the
2024-11-05 U.S. presidential election result) but differs structurally from the original
candidate's three-phase composite shape (chop -> sharp decline -> partial recovery): this wave is
closer to a single, more sustained directional decline (the "sustained multi-hour decline" family's
shape) punctuated by sharp volatile legs, rather than the original's build-up/resolution/retracement
pattern. Notably, the market's reaction to this scheduled political catalyst did not conclude
overnight as the original candidate assumed -- it continued into, and in fact intensified during,
the following daytime session, ultimately producing a larger net decline than the overnight portion
alone. This is treated as an addendum (continuation/revision of the same event's documented extent)
rather than a new Discovery Candidate, since it is the same underlying catalyst and a plausible
continuation of the same mechanism, not a structurally distinct new phenomenon.

## Impact on Confidence

Confidence in the core observation (a major scheduled political catalyst can produce an
exceptionally large, multi-phase, multi-hour volatility episode) is strengthened -- the total
scale is now roughly double what was originally documented. The open question about repetition
opportunity (noted in the original candidate as limited, given the low-frequency nature of the
catalyst) is unaffected. New open question: does the initial ~1hr lull before the second wave
represent a genuine but temporary resolution (e.g. markets briefly pricing in an initial
read of results before a clearer/confirmed outcome triggers the larger second wave), or is the
lull incidental? This instance does not have external information about the actual election
outcome or its timeline and does not speculate further on the specific causal mechanism --only the
price/volume shape is documented.
