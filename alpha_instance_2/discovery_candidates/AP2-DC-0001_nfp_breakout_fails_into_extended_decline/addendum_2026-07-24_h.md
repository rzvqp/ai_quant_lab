# Addendum H to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0008
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into

## Observation

2024-09-27 (a **Friday**, but not the first Friday of the month -- no NFP), ~12:30-16:30 UTC.
Pre-episode baseline: price had been consolidating 2658-2665 through the morning (London session).

12:30 UTC: O2664.8 H2672.42 L2664.03 C2671.82, volume 7608 (vs. a same-morning baseline of
2600-4400) -- a sharp rally, not a decline, off the pre-episode base. 12:45 UTC extends further to
an episode-high **2674.35** (volume 7041) -- a genuine new high above the pre-episode range.

This reclaim then fails, but unlike most prior instances the decline here is unusually **long and
gradual** rather than a quick single-leg drop: from 13:00 UTC through 16:15 UTC (fourteen
consecutive M15 candles), price ground steadily lower in waves -- 2669 -> 2666 -> 2662 -> 2659 ->
a sharper leg down to 2649 (14:45 UTC, volume 7593) -> episode low **2644.79** (15:00 UTC, volume
8543) -> a brief bounce -> a re-test of the low area (2645.29 at 15:45 UTC) -> finally stabilizing
2651-2656 by 16:30 UTC as volume normalized (8543 -> 4235). Volume stayed elevated (4993-8543)
across essentially the **entire ~4-hour episode**, the longest sustained-elevated-volume stretch of
any instance except Addendum D.

Total magnitude: **-29.56pt** from the 12:45 reclaim high (2674.35) to the 15:00 low (2644.79).
Duration ~4 hours end to end (12:30-16:30 UTC) -- the second-longest instance after Addendum D
(~4.5hrs).

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-G

Ninth instance total of the core mechanism (new high fails, reverses into a larger decline that
overshoots the pre-breakout level -- 2644.79 vs. the 2658-2665 pre-episode base). Structurally, this
instance sits between the "single clean top into sustained decline" pattern (original
candidate/Addendum F) and the "multi-attempt failed-reclaim" pattern (Addenda D/E/G) -- here there
is only one initial reclaim high, but the subsequent decline itself unfolds as a long, multi-wave
grind (several intermediate bounces and re-tests within the overall down-move) rather than either a
single clean slide or a cluster of sharp bounce-and-fail attempts near the top. This is a third
distinct sub-shape for how the "fails into larger decline" phase can manifest, alongside the two
already documented -- reinforcing that the post-failure decline phase is itself variable in
internal structure across instances, while the overall mechanism (fail-then-overshoot) remains
constant. Also notable: this is now the **second instance on a non-NFP Friday** (after none
previously specifically noted as Friday-non-NFP), further reinforcing the "12:30 UTC US data slot,
any weekday" framing over any specific release-type or day-of-week link.

## Impact on Confidence
