## Observation

2024-11-13 (a Wednesday, genuine US CPI release), ~13:30-18:15 UTC. Pre-event baseline: a quiet
grind 2608-2611 through the morning (London session). Note: the release landed at 13:30 UTC rather
than the previously-observed 12:30 UTC slot, consistent with the 2024-11-03 US DST end shifting
scheduled-release timing by 1hr in UTC terms (see the 2024-11-01 weekend-gap checkpoint note).

13:30 UTC: O2608.52 H2618.85 L2608.52 C2609.39, volume 10541 (near-record, among the highest
single-candle volumes observed this replay) -- a sharp spike to a marginal new high (2618.85,
above the entire pre-event range) that fails within the same candle, closing near the open. This
is a textbook AP2-DC-0001 origin candle.

13:45-14:15 UTC: continued heavy volume (7295-8363), a genuine reclaim attempt extending to
2616.4 (14:00 UTC) before turning down decisively. From 14:30 UTC the decline becomes sustained
and severe: heavy volume (5724-8765) across roughly ten consecutive candles drives price from
~2610 down through successive lower lows -- 2601.09, 2593.65, 2587.38, 2584.76, 2579.78 -- to an
episode low of **2577.60** (17:15 UTC). Volume then fades progressively (5169 -> 3675) as price
stabilizes **2582-2585** by 18:15 UTC.

Total episode duration ~4.75hrs (13:30-18:15 UTC). Magnitude: spike high (2618.85) to episode low
(2577.60) = **~41.25pt decline**, among the largest instances observed; final settlement
(~2582-2585) sits **~25pt below** the pre-event baseline (2608-2611) -- a substantial overshoot,
comparable in scale to Addendum D's ~44pt undershoot.

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-K

Thirteenth instance total of the core mechanism, and the second instance tied to a genuine CPI
release (after the 2024-09-11 Observation Registry entry). Notably, this instance CONTRASTS with
that earlier CPI instance: 2024-09-11 began with a sharp DECLINE (not a breakout) at the data slot
and was therefore filed as a distinct Observation Registry entry rather than an AP2-DC-0001
addendum; this 2024-11-13 instance instead begins with the canonical up-spike-then-fail shape,
matching AP2-DC-0001 cleanly. This is the same pattern already observed across FOMC instances
(2024-09-18 decline vs. 2024-11-07 hold) and reinforces that even holding the specific catalyst
type constant (CPI, in this case) does not determine which of the several possible reaction shapes
(decline-first CPI-style, breakout-then-fail AP2-DC-0001-style, or breakout-then-hold) will occur.

Structurally, the post-failure decline here most resembles the original candidate and Addendum F
(the largest instance yet, -53.2pt at a FOMC event): a single clean top followed by a large,
sustained, heavy-volume decline without the multi-attempt reclaim battles seen in Addenda D/E/G.
