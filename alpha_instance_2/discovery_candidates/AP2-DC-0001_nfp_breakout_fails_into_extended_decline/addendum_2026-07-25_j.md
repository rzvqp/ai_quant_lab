# Addendum J to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-25
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0010
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into

## Observation

2024-11-01 (the first Friday of November -- a genuine NFP release, same calendar catalyst as the
original AP2-DC-0001 instance, Addendum D, and Addendum I's Phase 2), ~12:30-16:00 UTC. Pre-event
baseline: a quiet overnight/morning grind 2747-2754 (Asia/early-London session).

12:30 UTC: O2750.01 H2759.89 L2749.05 C2755.94, volume 9599 (near-record) -- a sharp two-way
breakout candle, pushing to a marginal new high (2759.89) above the pre-event baseline and closing
near the high. 12:45 UTC pulls back to 2753.87 (volume 7487), then 13:00 UTC extends to a fresh
high **2761.49** (volume 8370, closing near the high at 2761.08).

13:15 UTC pushes to the episode high **2762.22** but reverses hard within the same candle, closing
2753.67 (volume 7551) -- a failed reclaim attempt. 13:30 UTC bounces back to 2758.49 (volume 8296),
13:45 UTC pulls back again to 2755.85 (volume 6408), 14:00 UTC churns two-way closing 2756.65
(volume 8448) -- three distinct high-area attempts (2759.89 / 2761.49 / 2762.22) within the first
90 minutes, each failing to hold.

From 14:15 UTC the decline turns decisive: 14:15 UTC closes 2751.79 (volume 7790), then 14:30 UTC
drops sharply to a low of 2739.73, closing 2739.83 (volume 8655) -- now clearly below the entire
pre-event baseline (2747-2754). A partial bounce to 2747.25 (14:45 UTC, volume 8303) is followed by
consolidation 2742-2750 through 15:00-16:00 UTC as volume fades progressively (6937 -> 5697 ->
6030 -> 4972), settling around 2743-2745 by 16:00 UTC.

Total episode duration ~3.5hrs (12:30-16:00 UTC). Episode high 2762.22, episode low 2739.73 (net
~22.5pt swing top-to-bottom); final settlement (~2743-2745) sits below the pre-event baseline
(2747-2754) but only modestly, not a dramatic overshoot as seen in some prior instances (e.g.
Addendum D's ~44pt undershoot).

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-I

Eleventh instance total of the core mechanism, and the third instance tied to a genuine NFP Friday
(after the original candidate and Addendum D; Addendum I's Phase 2 was also NFP-day but embedded in
a compound decline-first/breakout-first session). Structurally this instance most closely
resembles the "multi-attempt failed-reclaim" pattern (Addenda D/E/G): three distinct new-high
attempts within the first 90 minutes before the decline sets in, rather than a single clean top.
Notable distinguishing feature: the overshoot below the pre-event baseline is comparatively modest
here (~5-10pt below baseline at settlement) compared to the larger undershoots in Addenda D
(~44pt), H (~14-20pt), and the original candidate -- suggesting the magnitude of the post-failure
overshoot is itself variable across instances and not tightly coupled to how many failed-reclaim
attempts precede it (three attempts here, yet a comparatively mild final overshoot).

## Impact on Confidence

Eleven instances now, zero contradicting instances. Confidence in the core mechanism remains High.
Reinforces the open question already noted in Addendum H: the post-failure decline phase (both its
internal shape and its final overshoot magnitude) varies substantially across instances with no
identified predictor, even when the pre-failure structure (number of failed-reclaim attempts) is
similar to previously-documented instances (Addenda D/E).
