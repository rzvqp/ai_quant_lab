# Addendum E to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0005
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into
A Larger, Extended Decline That Overshoots The Pre-Breakout Level)

## Observation

2024-09-17 (a **Tuesday** -- not a Thursday-jobless-claims or Friday-NFP day, unlike every prior
instance of this mechanism), ~12:30-16:15 UTC. Pre-episode baseline: price had been consolidating
2576-2582 since ~11:30 UTC, closing the 12:15 candle at 2576.77.

12:30 UTC: O2576.77 H2579.03 L2568.41 C2573.33, volume 8489 (vs. a same-morning baseline of
2000-3500) -- an immediate sharp decline to 2568.41 rather than an upside breakout, followed by a
partial recovery into the close.

What follows is a **three-attempt failed-reclaim structure**, directly matching the nuance first
documented in Addendum D:
- Attempt 1 (13:00-13:15 UTC): recovers to marginal new highs 2579.07 then 2579.18 (essentially
  re-testing the pre-episode ceiling), fails, closes back down to 2575.35-2576.52.
- Attempt 2 (13:45 UTC): recovers again to a marginal new high 2579.6, fails again, low 2573.58.
- Attempt 3 (14:00-14:15 UTC, the largest and most convincing): a strong reclaim candle
  (O2573.75 H2581.11 C2580.8, volume 5998) decisively breaks above every prior level in the
  episode *and* the pre-episode baseline, extending further to an episode-high 2582.22 on the next
  candle -- genuinely resembling a successful breakout, not just a marginal poke.

This third attempt then fails just as hard as the first two: 14:30 UTC breaks to a new low 2571.8
(below all prior episode lows except the very first spike), and after one brief bounce (14:45,
2576.49) the decline resumes and accelerates, breaking decisively below the *entire episode's*
starting low (2568.41 -> 2567.15 at 15:15, continuing to a final low of 2561.82 at 15:30) before
stabilizing 2562-2565 through 16:15 UTC as volume faded back to baseline (3300-3800).

Total magnitude: -20.4pt from the episode-high reclaim (2582.22) to the final low (2561.82), over
~3h45m (12:30-16:15 UTC). Volume stayed elevated (3300-8500) for the entire episode, fading only in
the final 1-2 candles as price stabilized -- the same volume profile as every prior instance.

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-D

Sixth instance total of the core mechanism (marginal/sharp new high(s) fail into a materially
larger, overshooting decline). Directly reinforces the multi-attempt failed-reclaim nuance first
seen in Addendum D (2024-09-06), but with an important variation: here the *largest* reclaim
attempt (attempt 3, 2581.11-2582.22) genuinely broke above the pre-episode baseline and every
earlier level in the episode -- a more convincing "successful breakout" appearance than any single
attempt in Addendum D -- before failing just as completely as the smaller attempts. This suggests
the multi-attempt structure is not simply "several weak pokes at the same ceiling," but can include
a fully convincing breakout-looking attempt among the failures, which strengthens (rather than
weakens) the underlying mechanism: even a fully convincing reclaim did not prevent the eventual
larger overshoot.

Also notable: this is the **first instance to occur on a Tuesday**, with no Thursday-jobless-claims
or Friday-NFP framing available. Combined with Addendum A (no identified calendar catalyst at all)
and Addenda B/C (Thursday jobless-claims) and Addendum D (Friday NFP), the mechanism has now been
observed on four different day-of-week contexts. This further weakens any specific-release-type
link and strengthens the general "12:30 UTC US data slot, any weekday" framing over a
release-type-specific one.

## Impact on Confidence

Six instances now, zero contradicting instances. Confidence in the core mechanism remains High.
Reinforced open question from Addendum D (does a multi-attempt failed-reclaim structure
characterize the largest-magnitude instances specifically?) -- this instance's magnitude (~20pt) is
mid-pack among the six (larger than Addenda A-C, smaller than Addendum D's ~44pt), yet still shows
the multi-attempt structure, weakening the idea that multi-attempt structure is exclusive to the
very largest instances; it may instead be a general feature of this mechanism that is simply easier
to see clearly (more candles, more data) in the larger instances.
