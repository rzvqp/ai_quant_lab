# Addendum I to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0009
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into
A Larger, Extended Decline That Overshoots The Pre-Breakout Level)

## Observation

2024-10-04, ~12:30-16:15 UTC (the first Friday of October -- a genuine NFP release, same calendar
catalyst as the original AP2-DC-0001 instance and Addendum D). Pre-event baseline: a quiet grind
2657-2662 through the morning (London session).

Unlike every NFP/jobless-claims instance logged so far (all of which begin with an UP breakout),
this instance begins with a sharp DECLINE, resembling the CPI-day shape (2024-09-11 entry) rather
than the typical NFP pattern: 12:30 UTC candle O2656.25 H2656.47 L2637.86 C2640.96, volume 10580
(second-largest single-candle volume observed by this instance, after only Addendum D's 12640).
12:45 UTC extends the decline further to an episode low **2632.02** (O2640.97 C2638.86, volume
9734) -- roughly 25-29pt below the pre-event baseline.

From 13:00 UTC price recovers steadily (volume fading 7960 -> 6242) through 14:15 UTC, consolidating
2648-2655, still below the pre-event baseline. Then, starting ~14:30 UTC, the recovery accelerates
and genuinely **overshoots the pre-event baseline to a fresh episode high of 2670.2** (15:00 UTC
candle, O2658.47 C2666.62, volume 8186) -- a marginal new high above the entire pre-event range,
structurally identical to a fresh AP2-DC-0001-style breakout forming mid-episode. This new high then
fails immediately: the 15:15 UTC candle reverses within itself (high 2670.14 -> low 2657.1, close
2658.1, volume 8154) and the following candles decline hard (15:30 UTC low 2649.3, volume 7761),
continuing to grind lower with fading-but-still-elevated volume (6975 -> 5349 -> 3922) until
stabilizing around **2643-2649** by 16:15 UTC -- below both the second-phase high (2670.2) AND the
original pre-event baseline (2657-2662).

Total episode duration ~4hrs (12:30-16:15 UTC), near-record volume (10580 peak) sustained for the
first ~2.25hrs before fading.

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-H

This is a structurally novel, **compound** instance: rather than a single clean breakout-then-
failure, the episode unfolds in two distinct phases. Phase 1 (12:30-14:15 UTC) is a sharp DECLINE-
first shape resembling the CPI-day family (2024-09-11 entry) rather than the typical NFP/jobless-
claims up-breakout pattern -- the market recovers from this phase back toward, but not above, the
pre-event baseline. Phase 2 (14:30-16:15 UTC) is then a textbook instance of the core AP2-DC-0001
mechanism in miniature: a genuine marginal new high (2670.2, above the entire pre-event range) forms
and fails within 1-2 candles, reversing into a decline that settles below the pre-breakout baseline.

Tenth instance total of the core mechanism (counting Phase 2 as its own occurrence), and the second
instance tied to a genuine NFP Friday (after Addendum D). Notable: this is the first instance where
a CPI-style decline-first reaction and a classic AP2-DC-0001 breakout-failure occur back-to-back
within the same ~4-hour session -- suggesting the two previously-separate "families" (decline-first
vs. breakout-first) are not mutually exclusive at a single data-release event, and a session can
transition from one shape into the other.

## Impact on Confidence

Confidence in the core mechanism remains High (Phase 2 is an unambiguous instance). New open
question: does a decline-first reaction at a data-release slot increase the likelihood of a
subsequent breakout-failure attempt within the same session, or is the sequencing here coincidental?
