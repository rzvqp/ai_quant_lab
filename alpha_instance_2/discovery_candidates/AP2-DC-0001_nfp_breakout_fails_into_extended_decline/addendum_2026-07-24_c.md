## Observation

2024-09-05, ~12:15-16:30 UTC (a Thursday). A quiet overnight/morning grind (2506-2517, ordinary
volume 1984-4387) is interrupted at 12:15 UTC: O2513.66 H2520.86 L2513.66 C2518.88 (7.2pt range),
volume 8417 (~2-3x the immediate baseline). The breakout continues to a marginal new high of
2523.49 at 12:30 UTC (vol 8181) before failing: over the next ~2.5 hours the market declines
continuously on sustained, non-fading volume (6509, 8099, 5916, 7442, 7112, and a peak of 10095 at
14:00 UTC -- the largest volume in the entire sequence) to a low of 2503.97 by ~15:00 UTC --
roughly 19.5 points below the breakout peak and below the pre-breakout baseline (2506-2517).
Volume only begins fading in the final 2-3 candles (5267, 4844, 3785, 3494) as price stabilizes at
2506-2510 by 16:30 UTC, well below the pre-breakout level.

reclaiming it. Like Addendum B, this instance occurs at 12:15-12:30 UTC on a Thursday -- the same
jobless-claims timing already documented -- strengthening the "12:30 UTC US data slot" calendar
link further (now 3 of 4 total instances tied to that slot specifically: the original NFP-Friday
instance, Addendum B's jobless-claims-Thursday instance, and this one).
