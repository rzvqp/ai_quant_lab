# Addendum A to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0001
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into
A Larger, Extended Decline That Overshoots The Pre-Breakout Level)

## Observation

2024-08-15, roughly 08:30-10:15 UTC. Following an ordinary quiet grind (2472-2478, volume
2271-3620), one candle produces a sharp upside wick to a marginal new local high: O2472.2
H2479.82 L2462.53 C2470.51 (17.29pt range), volume 11541 (~3-4x the immediate baseline). The high
(2479.82) barely exceeds the immediately preceding local high (2478.69, made a few candles
earlier in the same session) before failing completely within the same candle -- the close
(2470.51) is well below both the high and the open.

What followed over the next ~8-9 candles (roughly 2 hours): a sustained decline with continuously
elevated volume (5928-9468, not fading), through 2468.82 -> 2465.72 -> 2450.92 -> 2458.04 ->
2453.57 -> 2449.14 -> ... settling at 2438-2445 (volume back to an ordinary 3297-5387) by
~10:15 UTC. Net decline from the peak: roughly 38 points.

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001

The original candidate's mechanism: a sharp breakout to a fresh high fails completely, and the
subsequent decline is materially LARGER than the initial breakout, overshooting the pre-breakout
level. This instance matches that shape closely: an upside push to a marginal new high fails
within the same candle, followed by a decline (~38pt) far exceeding the initial breakout's own
range (17.29pt), continuing well past where the move started. Differences from the original: this
instance's initial push barely exceeded the prior local high (2479.82 vs 2478.69, a marginal
breakout) rather than a clean, larger breakout; there is no identified calendar catalyst analogous
to NFP timing for this instance (2024-08-15 is a Thursday, no specific scheduled-release timing
identified from price action alone).

## Impact on Confidence

This is the second instance of this mechanism observed by this instance, independent of any
calendar catalyst -- suggesting the "extreme/marginal-new-high wick fails into a much larger,
extended decline" shape may not depend on NFP-specific timing, weakening that part of the original
candidate's framing (Section 3, "Why It May Repeat") while strengthening the core mechanism itself
(a general failed-breakout-into-larger-reversal pattern, independent of the specific calendar
catalyst). Confidence in the underlying mechanism moves from Medium toward Medium-High; confidence
in the NFP-specific timing link is now weaker, since this instance had no identified scheduled
catalyst.
