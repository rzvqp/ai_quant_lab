# Addendum D to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0004
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into
A Larger, Extended Decline That Overshoots The Pre-Breakout Level)

## Observation

2024-09-06, ~12:30-17:00 UTC (the first Friday of September -- a genuine NFP release, the same
calendar catalyst as the original AP2-DC-0001 instance). A quiet grind (2513-2519, ordinary volume)
is broken at 12:30 UTC: O2516.39 H2529.21 L2507 C2524.9 (22.2pt range), volume 12451 -- the
second-largest single-candle volume observed by this instance across the entire replay (after only
this same candidate's origin instance, 12999).

Unlike every prior instance of this mechanism, the reversal here is not a single clean decline --
it is a prolonged, multi-hour whipsaw with repeated failed reclaim attempts: after the initial
12:30 spike, price declines hard (13:00-13:15, low 2506.68), attempts a reclaim (13:30-13:45,
2511.13), fails again (14:00-14:15, low 2502.52), attempts a SECOND reclaim (14:30, spiking to
2515.64), fails again (14:45, low 2505.84), attempts a THIRD reclaim (15:00, spiking to 2518.48 on
volume 12640 -- the single largest candle volume observed in this instance's entire replay to
date), then fails decisively and declines continuously through 16:00-16:45 UTC to a final low of
2485.15 -- roughly 44 points below the 12:30 peak and well below the pre-breakout baseline
(2513-2519). Volume stayed elevated (7500-12640) for the entire ~4.5-hour episode, only fading in
the final 1-2 candles as price stabilized at 2492-2496.

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A/B/C

Fifth instance total of the same core mechanism (marginal/sharp new high fails into a materially
larger, overshooting decline), and the first instance since the origin candidate itself to occur on
a genuine NFP Friday (first Friday of the month) with a comparably large single-candle volume
spike. This instance is the largest in total magnitude (~44pt peak-to-trough) and longest in
duration (~4.5hrs) of all five instances, and is structurally richer than the others: rather than
one clean failed breakout, it shows THREE distinct failed reclaim attempts before the decline
resolves, each attempt itself carrying elevated volume (10707, 12640) rather than the reversal
being a single continuous slide. This suggests the mechanism, on its largest/most violent
instances, can manifest as a multi-attempt battle rather than a single clean failure -- a
structural nuance not previously captured in the candidate's Observation or Confidence sections.

## Impact on Confidence

Five instances now, zero contradicting instances. Confidence in the core mechanism remains High and
is further strengthened by direct confirmation on a genuine NFP Friday (matching the origin
candidate's exact calendar framing) with near-record volume. New nuance for future investigation:
does decline magnitude/duration correlate with the number of failed reclaim attempts observed
before final resolution, and is this multi-attempt structure itself a distinguishing feature of the
largest-magnitude instances specifically?
