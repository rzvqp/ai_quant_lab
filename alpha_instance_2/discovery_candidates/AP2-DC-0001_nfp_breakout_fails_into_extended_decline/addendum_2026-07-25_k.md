**Date**: 2026-07-25

**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into

## Observation

2024-11-08 (a Friday, but the second Friday of the month -- not NFP), ~12:30-17:15 UTC. Pre-event
baseline: a quiet grind 2688-2694 through the morning (London session).

12:30 UTC: O2691.92 H2692.07 L2687.89 C2691.12, volume 6515 -- a modest initial reaction. Unlike
most instances, the reclaim/extension here is gradual rather than an immediate sharp breakout:
over the following ~2 hours (12:45-14:15 UTC), price grinds higher with sustained elevated volume
(6616-6809) to an episode high of **2704.1** (14:15 UTC) -- a genuine new high, ~13-16pt above the
pre-event baseline.

From 14:30 UTC the reclaim fails: a sharp decline candle (vol 7851, low 2691.36) followed by
continued heavy two-way action (vol 6391-8195) breaking below the pre-event baseline to a low of
**2682.90** (16:15 UTC) -- the episode low. Volume then fades progressively (5632 -> 3888 -> 4798)
as price settles **2683-2687** by 17:15 UTC -- below the pre-event baseline (2688-2694) but only
modestly (~5-8pt), not a dramatic overshoot.

Total episode duration ~4.75hrs (12:30-17:15 UTC), sustained elevated-to-heavy volume for most of
that span (5300-8195 across the bulk of the episode).

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-J

Twelfth instance total of the core mechanism. Structurally distinctive in its build-up phase: most
prior instances show a sharp single-candle or few-candle breakout at or near 12:30 UTC, whereas
this instance's reclaim/extension unfolds gradually over ~2 hours before topping out -- a slower
build-up than any previously documented instance. The failure/decline phase itself resembles the
original candidate and Addendum F (a relatively clean decline rather than a prolonged multi-wave
grind), though punctuated by one sharp spike-down candle (vol 8195) mid-decline.

Notably, like Addendum J (2024-11-01), the final overshoot below the pre-event baseline is modest
(~5-8pt) rather than dramatic -- now the second consecutive Friday instance with this
comparatively mild undershoot characteristic, worth tracking as a possible (tentative, two-instance)
pattern: does clustering in early November specifically, or some other factor, correlate with
milder overshoots than earlier-observed instances (Addenda D, H)?

## Impact on Confidence

Twelve instances now, zero contradicting instances. Confidence in the core mechanism remains High.
The gradual (rather than sharp) build-up phase in this instance is a new build-up sub-shape,
alongside the already-documented variability in the post-failure decline phase -- reinforcing that
both halves of the mechanism (build-up and failure/decline) show internal shape variability while
the overall fail-then-overshoot mechanism itself remains constant across all twelve instances.
