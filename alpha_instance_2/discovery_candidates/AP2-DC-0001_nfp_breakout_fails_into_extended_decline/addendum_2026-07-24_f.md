# Addendum F to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0006
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into
A Larger, Extended Decline That Overshoots The Pre-Breakout Level)

## Observation

2024-09-18 (a **Wednesday** -- a scheduled FOMC rate-decision day, a fixed public macro-calendar
fact rather than anything read off the chart itself; statement/press-conference timing on the
US calendar typically falls ~18:00-18:30 UTC in September under EDT), ~18:00-20:45 UTC. Pre-episode
baseline: price had been consolidating 2570-2575 since ~16:00 UTC, closing the 17:45 candle at
2567.34 on ordinary volume (2700-3700).

18:00 UTC: O2567.51 H2591.46 L2566.42 C2588.7, volume 11250 -- an immediate, powerful breakout
upward (not a decline as in Addendum E), closing near the candle's high. This is the largest single-
candle volume yet observed by this instance except for Addendum D's 12451/12640 peaks.

Unlike every prior instance, the reversal here did **not** follow a multi-attempt failed-reclaim
structure (Addenda D/E). Instead:
- The breakout **extended cleanly for two more candles** (18:15-18:30: high 2597.9, volume 9913;
  18:30-18:45: a decisive new episode-high 2600.12 -- a genuine new period high, not a marginal poke
  -- on volume 10567), reaching a peak +33pt above the pre-episode baseline.
- The 18:30-18:45 candle then reverses hard **within the same candle**, closing at 2580.77, and from
  there the decline is **continuous and uninterrupted** for six more candles (18:45-20:00 UTC) with
  no bounce-and-fail reclaim attempts at all: 2575.77 -> 2565.89 (breaking below the entire
  pre-episode baseline) -> 2565.58 -> 2552.59 -> 2548.98, bottoming at a final low of **2546.89**
  (19:45-20:00 UTC).
- Volume stayed at extraordinary, sustained levels (8219-11265) across **eight consecutive
  candles** (18:00-20:00 UTC, exactly 2 hours) before fading sharply (4375 -> 3193 -> 2038) as
  price stabilized 2554-2559 through 20:45 UTC.

Total magnitude: **-53.2pt** from the episode-high (2600.12) to the final low (2546.89) -- the
**largest instance of this mechanism observed by this instance to date**, exceeding Addendum D's
~44pt. Duration ~2h45m from breakout start to stabilization, with the most sustained
extreme-volume stretch (8 consecutive candles above 8000, vs. isolated 1-2 candle peaks in earlier
instances) of any instance so far.

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addenda A-E

Seventh instance total of the core mechanism (a sharp move to a new high fails and reverses into a
materially larger, overshooting decline). Notably, this instance's internal structure is closer to
the **original AP2-DC-0001 description** (a single clean failure into an extended decline) than to
the multi-attempt failed-reclaim nuance documented in Addenda D and E -- there is no repeated
back-and-forth battle at the top here, just one decisive new high followed by an uninterrupted
slide. This is an important data point for the open question raised in Addendum E: it shows the
multi-attempt structure is **not** a universal feature of large-magnitude instances -- the single
largest instance yet observed (this one) shows the *opposite* structure (one clean top, continuous
decline), while a mid-magnitude instance (Addendum E, ~20pt) showed three attempts, and the
second-largest instance (Addendum D, ~44pt) showed three attempts. Magnitude alone does not predict
which internal structure (single-top vs. multi-attempt) will occur.

Also notable: this is the first instance clearly associated with a *policy-decision* type calendar
event (FOMC) rather than a *scheduled-data-release* type event (NFP, jobless claims, CPI). The
initial directional impulse here was a genuine breakout **up** (not down, as in the CPI-associated
Addendum E instance, and not the mixed initial direction of earlier instances) -- consistent with
the idea floated in Addendum E that initial direction may depend on the type of release/event, though
with only one policy-decision instance so far this remains a single data point.

## Impact on Confidence

Seven instances now, zero contradicting instances. Confidence in the core mechanism remains High and
is further strengthened by the largest-magnitude, highest-sustained-volume instance observed to
date. Refines (rather than resolves) the open question from Addendum E: multi-attempt
failed-reclaim structure is not a function of magnitude alone -- it appears possible for the
mechanism to resolve via either a single clean top or a multi-attempt battle regardless of how large
the eventual decline turns out to be. Open question for future instances: does the initiating event
type (scheduled data release vs. policy decision) predict which internal structure (single-top vs.
multi-attempt) occurs, or is this simply instance-to-instance variation with no deeper driver?
