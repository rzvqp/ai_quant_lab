# Addendum B to AP2-DC-0001

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Local addendum ID**: AP2-ADD-0002
**Against**: AP2-DC-0001 (A Sharp Breakout On A First-Friday-Of-Month Session Fully Reverses Into
A Larger, Extended Decline That Overshoots The Pre-Breakout Level)

## Observation

2024-08-15, ~12:15-14:15 UTC (a Thursday). An organic grind-up (2454 -> 2470.11, the session's
marginal new high, over roughly 4 hours of ordinary volume 1546-5241) fails immediately at
12:30 UTC: the very next candle (O2466.53 H2466.53 L2450.49 C2453.29, 16pt range) declines hard,
volume 11152 (~2-4x the immediate baseline). The decline continues over the next two candles --
2441.15 (vol 9825), then a low of 2432.23 within the 2437.88-close candle (vol 10507) -- roughly
38 points below the peak. A sharp recovery candle follows (O2437.84 C2450.99, vol 9783), then the
market oscillates with continued elevated volume (7325-10094) before gradually settling at
2455-2459 (volume back to an ordinary 4322-6851) by ~14:15 UTC -- below the original peak (2470.11)
but well above the low (2432.23).

## Comparison to AP2-DC-0001 and Addendum A

Third instance of the same mechanism: a marginal new high fails immediately, and the subsequent
decline is materially larger than whatever preceded it, followed by a partial (not full) recovery.
Unlike Addendum A's instance (no identified catalyst), this instance's failure occurs at **12:30
UTC on a Thursday** -- the U.S. weekly initial jobless claims release is scheduled at 12:30 UTC
on Thursdays, a distinct recurring release from the first-Friday NFP timing of the original
candidate. This is the second of three instances now tied to 12:30 UTC specifically (the original
NFP-Friday instance, and now this jobless-claims-Thursday instance), against one instance
(Addendum A) with no calendar link at all.

## Impact on Confidence

Three instances now: two at 12:30 UTC (different specific releases, same time-of-day), one with no
identified catalyst. This shifts the calendar-link question from "possibly NFP-specific" (as
originally framed) toward "possibly tied to the 12:30 UTC US data-release slot in general,
regardless of which specific release," while the no-catalyst instance (Addendum A) shows the
mechanism can also occur without any calendar link. Confidence in the core failed-breakout-into-
larger-reversal mechanism moves from Medium-High toward High (three consistent instances, no
contradicting instance yet observed). The calendar-timing question is not yet resolved: 12:30 UTC
appears in 2 of 3 instances, which is suggestive but not conclusive with this small a sample.
