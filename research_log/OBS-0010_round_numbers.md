# OBS-0010 — Do daily extremes cluster at round numbers?
Type: Observation Record. **NOT a Discovery Candidate.** Date 2026-07-22 · XAUUSD H1→daily · loader · 1,750 daily extremes.

**Q:** Do daily highs/lows cluster near round-number levels ($50 / $100 multiples), as commonly believed?
**Pre-reg:** share of extremes within ±10% of the grid vs the uniform expectation (0.20); clustering if CI95 > uniform.

**Result:** $50 grid share within ±$5 = **0.192** (CI[0.173,0.211]); $100 grid within ±$10 = **0.163** — both **at or BELOW** the uniform 0.20.

**Verdict: NEGATIVE (belief refuted).** XAUUSD daily extremes show **no** attraction to round numbers; if anything a slight avoidance at the $100 grid. Round-number magnetism is not a descriptive feature here.
**Residue:** none.
