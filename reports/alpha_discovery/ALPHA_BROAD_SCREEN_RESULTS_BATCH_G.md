## Decisive finding (R20)
**NO causal price STATE tested carries a stable, material, cross-era directional lean.** Every asymmetry is small (mostly ≤0.05) and, where present, **era-trend-dependent**: a SHORT lean in b0/b1 (2011-2018, incl. the big bear) that vanishes or flips in the 2021-2024 bull (CAL's small long-leans are just the 2024 bull). This is the era-trend-leakage signature (R16) proven at the INFORMATION level, before any strategy or cost.

**Interpretation — the "why" behind the whole campaign:** directional price information in XAUUSD *is* the era's own secular trend, which by construction does not generalize cross-era. Therefore every directional-STATE mechanism fails cross-era (Batches A-F), and S5's edge is robust precisely because it is a **structural / session-timing** bet (the NY opening-range breakout provides its own direction via the event), NOT a prediction from a market state. The directional-state information class is empty (§40 class-level conclusion).

## Frontier implication
A new price-only edge is unlikely to come from state-directional prediction (info absent). It must come, like S5, from **structural/timing events that supply their own direction** — and must be tested at the ECONOMIC-EVENT level for independence vs S5 (Batch D lesson). Session-anchored variants are S5-redundant; the open question is whether a NON-session structural/timing event exists that is both directional and independent. Continue there; volatility-timing (non-directional) remains a filter, not a standalone edge (R12).
