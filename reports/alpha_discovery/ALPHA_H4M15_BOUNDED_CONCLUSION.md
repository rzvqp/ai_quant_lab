## Central findings
1. **H4-conditioning worked as a lens.** It cleanly isolated ONE genuine directional mechanism — *DOWN-H4 + M15 short-continuation* — surfaced by three independent triggers (impulse-shallow, deceleration, structure+decel), and it reproduced the standalone-M15 volatility finding under a stricter same-state cross-era gate.
2. **The only cross-era-stable M15 information is VOLATILITY-STRUCTURE** (expansion → bilateral higher P(±X); contraction → bilateral avoidance) — **non-directional**. This exactly matches the standalone-M15 bounded conclusion, now re-derived with H4 conditioning.
3. **The one cross-era-stable-INFO directional mechanism is not a robust strategy:**
   - **Expectancy is era-conditional** — tradeable only in the high-volatility strong-downtrend era (b0: avgR +0.09..+0.20); net-negative in the mixed DEV era and the low-vol b1 era. Root cause: the DOWN-state base rate *and* absolute follow-through scale with era volatility (b1 median MFE 32p vs b0 59p), so a cross-era-stable **relative** hit-rate lift (+0.03..+0.06) converts to positive **expectancy** only where moves are large.
   - **Its triggers are mutually redundant** — F2 and F5 share 100% of trading days → one mechanism, not multiple strategies (§18).
4. **All other directional path-edges are era-conditional or inconsistent** (continuation via persistence; reversal/absorption; momentum-extreme; directional swing-structure all fail the b1 low-vol gate or DISC/CONF).
5. **Binding constraint: the b1 (2016-2018) low-volatility regime lacks the directional follow-through** that price-only path-shape directional edges require. Only volatility-structure — which is non-directional — is genuinely stationary.

## Bottom line
**No robust, non-redundant, cross-era-tradeable NEW price-only strategy module emerged.** The portfolio target (40–60 effective opps/mo across independent robust strategies) is not advanced by a new H4-M15 path-shape strategy. Frozen **S5** and **COMP-CONT-L-rr2** remain the robust price-only edges.

**Convergent meta-result (now across THREE independent methods — standalone M15, regime-conditional state→path, H4-conditioned path-shape):** on XAUUSD, cross-era-stable price-only *information* exists but is **volatility-structural and non-directional**; cross-era-stable directional *expectancy* does not exist in price-only features, because absolute follow-through scales with era volatility. Robust directional edges are inherently regime/era-specific (hence S5 and COMP-CONT-L are the survivors).
