# GC OPEN INTEREST CONTEXT V1 — interpretation (post-freeze)

Unblinded only after the walk-forward, gates and controls were frozen.

## What the evidence says
Adding genuine daily CME GC total open interest (level/regime, change, and its interactions with GC price, GC volume, and the XAU setup state)
to the XAU baseline changed selected expectancy by at most +0.003…+0.006R at 60% winner retention across the three mechanisms — an order of
magnitude below the +0.05R information gate — and on the auction setup adding OI was marginally worse. Permuting the trade-specific OI within
month buckets (OI-destruction) did not degrade the result on any setup, so whatever tiny structure the OI features carried is not trade-specific
positioning information.

## The specific sub-questions
- **Does OI rescue the already-useless GC volume?** No. The volume×OI representation (F) beats the volume representation (C) by only +0.001…
  +0.006R — interpreting volume through positioning did not make it informative.
- **Price × OI** (new longs / short-covering style states, tested neutrally): +0.007R at best, negative on the auction setup — no winner-vs-loser value.
- **XAU/GC-relative × OI**: within the null.

## Honest framing
A well-powered null on a specific, pre-registered OI representation and three specific setups. GC open interest is a slow (daily, ~1-day-lagged)
positioning aggregate; here it does not condition the quality of these intraday XAU setups. Per §25 no directional-positioning claim ("new longs
entered") is asserted — the schema (level + change only, no long/short split) would not support it, and in any case no OI state separated
winners from losers. Nothing broader is claimed (not that OI is information-free in general).
