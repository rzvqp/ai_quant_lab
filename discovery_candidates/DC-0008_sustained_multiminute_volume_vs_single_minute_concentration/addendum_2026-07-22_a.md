## New Observation

Same session, same instrument (OANDA:XAUUSD), replay 2025-08-01. At **19:30 UTC**, an M15 candle
ran from 3351.175 to a high of 3361.045, closing 3360.33 (roughly 10pt, volume 9,844 versus a

12:30 NFP candle's construction type, not the 03:40 candle's: elevated volume across nearly every
one of the ~12-14 minutes from 19:30 to 19:42 (roughly 450-1,100 per minute vs a quieter
preceding baseline), price advancing in a steady staircase rather than one dominant minute.

## Why This Matters To DC-0008

Unlike the 12:30 candle, 19:30 UTC does not correspond to any obvious scheduled macro-release
slot. DC-0008's Section 3 offered a scheduled-news timing coincidence as one plausible (unverified)
source of the "sustained repricing" construction. This second instance of the same construction
type, without that timing coincidence, is a data point against timed-news being a necessary
condition for it — it does not rule scheduled news out as *a* cause on 12:30, but it shows the
construction type itself is not exclusive to that context. Offered descriptively; no causal claim
either way, and no attempt to explain what did drive the 19:30 move.
