## New Observation: A Fifth 12:30 UTC Instance — Sustained Construction Again, on a Second Friday (Not NFP)

On 2025-08-15 (a Friday, but the third Friday of August — not the first-Friday NFP slot referenced
in v1), the M15 candle 12:30-12:45 UTC ran O3337.425 H3343.845 L3333.6 C3343.045 — a 10.25-point
range, volume 9,180 (roughly 2-3x the immediately preceding M15 candles' 2,750-5,450).

Dropping to M5 then M1: volume was distributed fairly evenly across all three M5 candles
(2,757 / 2,937 / 3,486) and across essentially all fifteen M1 candles (371-942, no single dominant
minute) — this matches the "sustained multi-minute construction" from v1, not the single-minute
concentration seen in Addenda B and C. Price moved in a broadly steady climb from 3333.6 up to
3343.845 across the full window rather than one sharp displacement.

## Why This Matters To DC-0008

This is the fifth distinct 12:30 UTC instance across this replay:

- 2025-08-01 (Friday, NFP slot): **sustained** construction, extended to new highs.
- 2025-08-12 (Tuesday): single-minute **concentration** (upside), consolidated at new level.
- 2025-08-13 (Wednesday): fully ordinary.
- 2025-08-14 (Thursday): single-minute **concentration** (downside), choppy incomplete reclaim.
- 2025-08-15 (Friday, not NFP): **sustained** construction, distributed across the full 15 minutes.

Notably, both Friday instances (08-01 and 08-15) showed sustained construction, while all three
weekday instances (Tue/Wed/Thu) showed either concentration or nothing. With only two Fridays
observed, this is not enough to establish a day-of-week effect, but it is a pattern worth flagging:
the original hypothesis in v1 tied the 08-01 sustained construction specifically to the NFP
calendar slot; this instance suggests the correlation (if real) may run with the day of the week
more broadly, or with something else common to Fridays, rather than being NFP-specific. This
remains an open, unverified observation — two data points is not a basis for a claim, only a
direction for future comparison.
