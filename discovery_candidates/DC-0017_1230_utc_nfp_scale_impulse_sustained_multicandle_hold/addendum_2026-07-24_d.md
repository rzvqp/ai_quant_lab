## New Observation

On 2025-10-24 — a Friday, but **not** the first Friday of the month, and therefore not consistent
with a scheduled NFP-type release — the 12:30 UTC hour produced an impulse that **sets a new
magnitude record for this family's non-NFP instances**, exceeding even Addendum C's previous
non-NFP record. Price rose from 4061.27 (12:15-12:30 UTC low) to a peak of 4135.02 (14:00-14:15
UTC) — a **73.75-point move**, well above Addendum C's 48.85pt (2025-10-20, also non-NFP) and
approaching the original NFP-day instance's 33.15pt from the opposite direction of scale. The move
unfolded over roughly nine M15 candles (12:15-15:15 UTC, ~3 hours) on a volume band of 8,974-10,953
— within the family's established range, comparable to Addendum C's 9.9-10.6k and to the original's
much larger 30,975 only in signature, not magnitude.

Unlike Addendum C's clean "hold" resolution, this instance resolved the way Addendum B's did:
after the peak, price gave back roughly half the move in choppy, bidirectional trading (peak
4135.02 down to 4112.31, back up to 4131.19, down again, etc.) rather than holding cleanly near the
highs, before volume finally dropped below 8,000 and price settled around 4113-4128 — well above
the 12:15 starting level but well below the peak. Dropping to M5 confirmed the same distributed,
non-concentrated construction already documented for this family: volume per 5-minute bar ranged
2,156-3,802 across the visible window with no single dominant bar.

## Why This Matters To DC-0017

This is the second consecutive non-NFP instance (after Addendum C) to set a new magnitude record
for this family without NFP-scale volume, further weakening any characterization that ties
magnitude at this hour to NFP-scale participation. It also replicates Addendum B's finding that
magnitude alone does not determine resolution style: this instance, despite exceeding Addendum C's
magnitude, resolved via extended chop rather than a clean hold — matching Addendum B's "second
largest volume instance resolved via bidirectional chop, not hold" pattern rather than Addendum C's
clean-hold pattern. Combined, addenda B, C, and D show that at this hour, magnitude, volume, and
resolution style vary largely independently of each other and of NFP status, with n too small
(3 addenda) to characterize a stable relationship between them.
