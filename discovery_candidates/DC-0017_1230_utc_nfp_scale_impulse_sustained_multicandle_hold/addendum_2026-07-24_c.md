# Addendum C to DC-0017 (dated 2026-07-24)

Filed per the Handoff Statement of `candidate_v1.md` — new evidence, not an edit to the frozen

## New Observation

On 2025-10-20 — a Monday, **not** the first Friday of the month and therefore not consistent with a
scheduled NFP-type release — the 12:30 UTC hour produced an impulse-then-hold construction that
**exceeds the original NFP-scale instance in magnitude while running at roughly one-third its
volume**. Price rose from 4278.42 (12:15-12:30 UTC low) to a peak of 4327.27 (12:45-13:00 UTC) — a
**48.85-point move**, larger than the original candidate's 33.15pt NFP-day impulse — over four M15
candles, on volumes of 10111, 10574, 9875, 10281. This volume band sits comfortably within the
"ordinary" 12:30 UTC envelope the original document describes (4,500-14,000, drawn from 22 prior
ordinary instances), far below the original's 30,975. Dropping to M5 confirmed the same sustained,
broadly distributed multi-minute construction already documented for this family: volume per
5-minute bar ranged 1,518-3,706 with no single dominant bar, gradually rising as the impulse
accelerated (from ~1,500-1,800 in the lead-up to 3,300-3,700 during the climb) — the same signature
as the original's M1 breakdown, just at 5-minute resolution and roughly a third of the absolute
volume.

Following the peak, price held its gains across the next several M15 candles — oscillating within
a 4313-4327 band — rather than reversing back toward the pre-impulse ~4278 level or extending
dramatically further, with volume gradually declining (10281, 8245, and below) as the hold matured.
This is the same "impulse-then-hold" resolution shape documented in the original candidate.

## Why This Matters To DC-0017

The original candidate's Section 3 speculated that "if 12:30 UTC first-Friday timing does
correspond to a scheduled macro release, similar-magnitude instances would be expected to recur on
a monthly cadence" — implicitly tying magnitude to the NFP-scale volume signature. This instance
weakens that implicit link: an ordinary Monday, at ordinary-range volume, produced a *larger* net
displacement than the original NFP-day instance. This parallels the volume/magnitude decoupling
already documented in DC-0013's Addendum B (a comparable-or-larger displacement at markedly lower
volume than the family's original high-volume instances) — suggesting that, across multiple
"large sustained expansion" family members, magnitude is not reliably predicted by volume alone,
and that the 12:30 UTC hour specifically can produce NFP-scale-or-larger price displacement without
NFP-scale volume or (presumably) an NFP-scale catalyst. The "impulse-then-hold" resolution shape,
however, replicated faithfully, suggesting that resolution style may be a more durable signature of
this construction than either volume or magnitude.

This is a single instance (n=1 for a non-NFP-day impulse of this magnitude) and does not establish
that ordinary 12:30 UTC instances regularly exceed NFP-day magnitude — only that they can.
