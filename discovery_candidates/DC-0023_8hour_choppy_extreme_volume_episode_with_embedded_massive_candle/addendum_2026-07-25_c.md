## New Observation

Continuing directly from Addendum B (which documented this same episode reaching a peak volume of
48,401, then a further marginal peak of 49,448 noted but not separately addended), the episode
extended and intensified substantially further on 2026-01-30, spanning roughly 14:15-22:00 UTC
(~7h45m) before the weekend gap. Peak candles from this extension:

| Candle (UTC) | Volume |
|---|---|
| 15:00-15:15 | **53,154** |
| 15:45-16:00 | 48,141 |
| 16:00-16:15 | 50,599 |
| 16:15-16:30 | 50,809 |
| 17:00-17:15 | 52,558 |

The peak candle (15:00-15:15 UTC, **53,154**) is a **new all-time single-candle volume record**,
exceeding this episode's own previous peak (48,401, Addendum B) by 4,753 (+9.8%), and the
previously-noted marginal peak (49,448) by 3,706 (+7.5%) — this time a clear, decisive new record
rather than a marginal/noise-level increment. Four separate candles in this window exceed 48,000,
each individually surpassing the record this candidate held prior to Addendum B.

Dropping to M5 on all four of the largest candles confirms organic construction: 53,154 splits
19,514/17,277/16,363 (largest share 36.7%); 52,558 splits 17,642/17,589/17,327 (largest share
33.6%); 50,809 splits 17,809/15,751/17,249 (largest share 35.0%); 50,599 splits
16,106/16,303/18,190 (largest share 35.9%) — all comfortably below the 42.7% reference, and notably
even across all three sub-candles in every case, reinforcing genuine broad participation.

This extension of the episode also carries the largest total price displacement documented for this
mechanism to date — see **Addendum B to DC-0024**, filed separately for the magnitude-record aspect
of this same window (434.285pt, a new all-time magnitude record).

## Why This Matters To DC-0023

This is now the **largest single-candle volume record in this replay** (53,154), continuing the
pattern established by Addendum B of this mechanism's volume ceiling being repeatedly extended by
new instances. Combined with Addendum B to DC-0024 (documenting the same window's magnitude record),
this single continuous episode now holds this replay's all-time records on both the volume and
magnitude axes simultaneously — the first time one episode has held both records at once.
