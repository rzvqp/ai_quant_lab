## New Observation: The Same Mechanism In A Much More Compressed, Higher-Intensity Form

On 2025-12-12, following an ordinary multi-hour grind higher (peaking at 4353.555, ~15:00 UTC),
price reversed into a sustained decline carrying **eight consecutive M15 candles (15:15-17:00 UTC,
~1h45m) with volume never below 17,000** — a much higher sustained volume floor than v1's episode
(which ran 9,000-22,700 over 8 hours). Representative candles:

| Candle (UTC) | Volume |
|---|---|
| 15:15-15:30 | 33,999 |
| 15:30-15:45 | **35,082** |
| 15:45-16:00 | 25,332 |
| 16:00-16:15 | 34,156 |
| 16:15-16:30 | 24,666 |
| 16:30-16:45 | 29,478 |
| 16:45-17:00 | 22,079 |
| 17:00-17:15 | 17,697 |

The peak candle (15:30-15:45 UTC, 35,082) is the **second-highest single-candle volume observed
anywhere in this replay**, behind only DC-0020's 37,204. Dropping to M5 on the three largest
candles to confirm organic construction: 35,082 splits 13,059/11,484/10,539 (largest share 37.2%);
33,999 splits 9,674/12,522/11,803 (largest share 36.8%); 34,156 splits 11,444/11,698/11,014 (largest
share 34.2%) — all comfortably below the 42.7% concentration reference, confirming genuine sustained
participation rather than an artifact.

Total range: from the pre-decline peak (4353.555) to the episode low (4257.275, reached
16:30-16:45 UTC) = **96.28 points** — close to but below v1's 100.005pt and DC-0024's 125.685pt
records. Price partially recovered afterward (to ~4283-4290 by the end of the observed window),
volume decaying steadily as it did.

## Why This Matters To DC-0023

This is the same underlying mechanism (sustained, organically-distributed elevated volume across a
multi-candle episode, containing a near-record single-candle volume event) but demonstrates it can
also occur in a **far more compressed, higher-intensity form**: ~1h45m and 8 candles instead of 8
hours and 32, with every candle in the episode above 17,000 (compared to v1's floor of 9,000).
This directly speaks to the open question v1 raised in "Why It May Repeat" — whether the mechanism
is bound to a particular duration/intensity profile, or can manifest across a range of scales. This
instance shows a materially shorter, more concentrated variant.

Three-part novelty test applied explicitly (CEO directive) before deciding artifact type: (1) Is
this a new MECHANISM? No — it is the same sustained-elevated-volume, near-record-candle mechanism
documented in DC-0023 (and echoed in DC-0018/DC-0020/DC-0024), just compressed into a shorter,
more intense window. (2) Could this be filed only as an Addendum? Yes — that is exactly what this
document is; the episode's specific numbers (magnitude, peak volume) sit close to but below the
existing records set by DC-0020/DC-0023/DC-0024, which is the textbook case for an addendum rather
than a new claim. (3) Is this a new record? No — 35,082 ranks second all-time (below DC-0020's
37,204); the ~96.28pt magnitude is below both DC-0023's 100.005pt and DC-0024's 125.685pt.
