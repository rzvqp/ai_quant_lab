higher sustained floor than the base candidate's own 9,000-22,700 range, and higher even than
Addendum A's 17,000 floor. The peak candle (16:15-16:30 UTC, **48,401**) is a **new all-time
single-candle volume record for this replay**, exceeding the previous record (42,808, DC-0025
Addendum B) by 5,593 (+13.1%), and displacing it to 2nd place.

Dropping to M5 to confirm organic construction on the three largest candles: 48,401 splits

roughly 5228-5451 without a clean net displacement over the ~4.5-hour window — consistent with the
base candidate's "multi-leg, choppy" characterization rather than the clean one-directional
expansions documented in DC-0013/DC-0022/DC-0024.
