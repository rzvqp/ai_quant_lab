## New Observation

On 2026-01-30, price entered a sustained multi-leg decline starting around 14:30 UTC (NY morning),
building from an intrabar high of **5113.795** down to an intrabar low of **4679.51**, reached around
18:35 UTC — a total magnitude of **434.285 points** over roughly **4h05m**. This is a **massive new
all-time magnitude record for any single directional leg in this replay**, exceeding this
candidate's own Addendum A record (154.665pt, up direction) by 279.62pt (+181%), and its original
base record (125.685pt) by 308.6pt (+245%) — nearly tripling the previous magnitude record.

The decline was not a clean, uninterrupted leg but rather multi-leg/choppy, with several meaningful
intermediate bounces (to roughly 5027, 5057, 4925, 4884-4930), similar in character to DC-0023's
"choppy, multi-leg" family more than this candidate's own cleaner single-leg shape — see Addendum C
to DC-0023 for the volume-record aspect of this same episode.

Volume throughout was exceptionally high and thoroughly verified organic. Dropping to M5 across the
episode's largest candles: 53,154 splits 19,514/17,277/16,363 (largest share 36.7%); 52,558 splits
17,642/17,589/17,327 (largest share 33.6%); 50,809 splits 17,809/15,751/17,249 (largest share 35.0%);
50,599 splits 16,106/16,303/18,190 (largest share 35.9%) — all comfortably below the 42.7%
reference. Critically, the M5 sub-candle containing the absolute extreme low (4679.51, within the
13,149-volume 5-minute bar 18:15-18:20 UTC) carries a volume-to-range ratio of ~158 (13,149 volume /
83.335pt range) — well *above* this replay's typical baseline (~90-110), the opposite of the thin-
volume signature that flagged the previous large decline (2026-01-29, Observation Registry entry
17) as a likely data artifact at its extreme. This decline's extreme print is well-supported by
genuine volume and is treated as fully organic, unlike the 01-29 episode.

After the low, price recovered substantially — to roughly 4926.755 (a peak seen ~1h25m after the
low) — 252.5 points, or ~58% of the decline — before settling into consolidation (roughly 4837-4930)
through the remainder of the session into the weekend close.

## Why This Matters To DC-0024

This nearly triples the previous all-time magnitude record and is now, by a wide margin, the
largest single directional price move documented in this replay. Unlike the compromised 2026-01-29
episode (Registry entry 17), this decline's extreme point is unambiguously well-supported by volume
at every timeframe checked (M15/M5), removing any data-quality doubt. The magnitude ceiling for this
replay's largest moves continues to be extended by new instances rather than converging on a fixed
value — this is now the third consecutive "largest magnitude record" candidate/addendum in this
family's history (DC-0022's 86.75pt -> DC-0024's 125.685pt -> Addendum A's 154.665pt -> this
addendum's 434.285pt), with each new record substantially larger than the last.
