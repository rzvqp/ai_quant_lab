## New Observation

Following directly from the decline documented in Addendum B (low 4402.38 reached within the
2026-02-02 06:30-06:45 UTC candle, already noted in the prior checkpoint as an ordinary continuation
of a previously-observed decline, no new record at that time), price reversed and built a sustained,
multi-leg climb from that same low (**4402.38**, 06:30 UTC) up to an intrabar high of **4812.265**,
reached around 12:45 UTC — a total magnitude of **409.885 points** over roughly **6h15m** (25
candles).

This is a **new all-time magnitude record for the up direction**, exceeding this candidate's own
Addendum A (154.665pt, up direction) by 255.22pt (+165%). It remains below the all-time record for
either direction (434.285pt, down direction, Addendum B) but is now the second-largest single
directional move documented in this replay overall, and by a wide margin the largest up-direction
move.

The climb was multi-leg/choppy rather than a single clean advance, with several intermediate
pullbacks (from ~4650 to ~4603, from ~4734 to ~4661, and a brief dip mid-climb), similar in character
to this candidate's Addendum A and to DC-0023's "choppy, multi-leg" family. No embedded candle
approached this replay's volume record (max candle in this window: 47,068, well below the 53,154
all-time record) — this event's novelty is on the magnitude/duration axis, not volume.

Verified organic via M5 on the three most relevant candles: the low-forming candle (M15 volume
42,254) splits 11,930/14,624/15,700 (largest share 37.2%); the first major recovery candle (M15
volume 39,038) splits 12,285/13,938/12,815 (largest share 35.7%); the peak-forming candle (M15 volume

confirming genuine, distributed participation rather than a concentrated or artifact-like print at
either the low or the high.

After the high, price gave back a portion of the advance, declining to 4686.02 by 13:15 UTC (~126pt
retracement, ~31% of the climb) before this checkpoint's batch ended.
