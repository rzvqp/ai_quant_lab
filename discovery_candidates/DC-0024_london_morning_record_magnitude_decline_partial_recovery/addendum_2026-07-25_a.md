5157-5186), price began a sustained climb starting around 23:30 UTC that continued, largely without
a reversal exceeding ~15pt at any point, for roughly 32 M15 candles (~8h, 23:30 UTC 01-27 - 08:30 UTC

floor (5157, already logged) to this peak: **154.665 points** — a **new all-time magnitude record
for any single directional leg in this replay**, exceeding this candidate's own record (125.685pt,
down direction) by 28.98pt (+23%), and the first time this record has been set in the **up**
direction (the prior record, and DC-0022's before it, were both down-direction).

Unlike the base candidate's own resolution (a substantial but ordinary partial recovery after the
low), this climb ended in an abrupt, violent **reversal** rather than a gradual pullback: starting
around 15:00 UTC on 01-28, price fell from the peak-adjacent level (5298.35 intrabar high) to an
intrabar low of 5235.75 — 75.915pt — within just **2 M15 candles (30 minutes)**, with the sharpest
single 5-minute sub-candle alone accounting for ~58pt of that decline. This reversal is filed
separately as **Addendum A to DC-0025** (the fast-waterfall/record-volume family), since its
character (extreme velocity, new all-time volume record) matches that mechanism far more closely
than this candidate's own gradual-multi-candle-decline shape. It is cross-referenced here because it
is the resolution event for this record-setting climb.
