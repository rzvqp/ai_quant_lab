## New Observation

The base candidate documented a partial recovery in progress as of 01:45 UTC Monday
(2025-10-27), with price oscillating 4088-4098 after the sustained decline. Continued observation
through 06:15 UTC shows this recovery was **not durable**: price gave the recovery back entirely
and extended to a **fresh marginal low**. From the recovery peak (close 4093.7 at 4093.7, ~01:30
UTC), price declined over roughly 9 M15 candles (01:45-04:00 UTC) to an intrabar low of **4053.71**
and a closing low of 4057.645 (04:00-04:15 UTC bar) — **below the original session low** documented
in the base candidate (intrabar 4058.205 / close 4064.665). This makes the fresh low the new
magnitude record for the episode: **~60.4pt below Friday's pre-gap close (4114.125) intrabar**,
~56.5pt close-to-close, versus the ~55.9pt/~49.5pt documented at the time of writing.

Volume through this second decline leg ran similarly moderate to the first (M15: roughly 3.9k-7.4k;
M5 sub-bars building 815 -> 1126 -> 1984 -> 2452 -> 3313 into the low, then fading to 1684 — no
single M5 candle exceeding ~44% of its M15 parent). Dropping to M5 confirmed the same distributed,
organic construction already established for the first leg — not a data artifact.

After the fresh low, price recovered a second time, over roughly 8 candles (04:00-05:00 UTC) back
to 4081.89, then flattened into a tight consolidation (4076-4085, closes essentially flat at
4079.94/4079.87/4079.75) through the end of this observation window (06:15 UTC). This second
recovery is, so far, of similar scale to the first (~28pt vs. ~24pt) and has likewise stalled well
short of a full retracement to the pre-gap level (4114.125).

Full revised timeline: gap-open (22:00 UTC Sun) -> decline to first low (00:15 UTC Mon, ~2h) ->
stabilization -> partial recovery #1 (peak ~01:30 UTC) -> renewed decline to fresh marginal low
(04:00 UTC, ~2h15m) -> partial recovery #2 (~04:00-05:00 UTC) -> stabilization (05:00-06:15 UTC).
Total elapsed since gap-open: ~8h15m, ~33 M15 candles — a multi-leg event, not the single clean
decline-then-partial-recovery arc the base candidate described at the time it was written.

## Why This Matters To DC-0019

This changes the shape of the event from "decline, then a single partial recovery" to a **multi-leg
oscillation**: decline, partial recovery, renewed decline past the prior low, second partial
recovery, stabilization — structurally closer to DC-0013 Addendum H's extended multi-leg
oscillation (also an initial large move followed by repeated partial-recovery/renewed-decline legs
before final stabilization) than to a single clean event. That resemblance is noted only as a shape
comparison; DC-0013 Addendum H occurred in the NY session at 9-12k+ volume, while this event remains
in the Sunday-reopen/early-Asia session at sub-9k volume — the sessions and volume regimes differ,
so no claim is made that these are the same mechanism. It also means neither of the two recovery
attempts documented so far should be read as the event's resolution — as of this addendum, price
remains in a stabilization phase roughly 33-38pt below the pre-gap level, with the fresh low still
standing as the episode's extreme.
