## New Observation

On 2026-01-04, the 13th weekend-gap instance in this replay produced a **+24.44pt gap up**
(Friday's last M15 close 4332.065 -> Sunday reopen open 4356.505) — the second-largest gap
magnitude observed in this replay, behind only this DC's own record (28.43pt). As with the base
candidate, the gap did not retrace: price extended further in the gap's own direction (up, this
time) rather than filling back toward the pre-gap level. Over the following ~4h15m (23:00 UTC
Sunday -> ~01:15 UTC Monday, ~17 M15 candles), price climbed to an intrabar high of **4421.605**
(01:00-01:15 UTC) — **89.54pt above the pre-gap close**, exceeding even DC-0019's own final
extension magnitude (~60.4pt). Volume through the extension ran moderate-elevated (M15: roughly
5.2k-27.6k, no sustained near-record plateau); dropping to M5 on the peak-volume candle (27,561)
confirmed organic, distributed construction (10,101/9,289/8,171 sub-bars, largest share 36.65%,
below the 42.7% concentration reference) — not a data artifact.

After the peak, price gave back part of the extension and stabilized in a 4388-4421 range for the
remainder of the observed window (~01:15-04:00 UTC) — the same "extend, then consolidate without
retracing" resolution shape as the base candidate, but without (so far) a second decline leg past
the extreme the way DC-0019's own Addendum A documented.

## Why This Matters To DC-0019

This is the **first instance of this mechanism observed in the up direction** — DC-0019 and its
Addendum A both documented a down-gap that failed to retrace and extended further down. This
instance shows the same shape (large gap, no retrace, sustained extension in the gap's own
direction, eventual stabilization) mirrored for an up-gap, at a comparable-but-smaller gap
magnitude (24.44pt vs. 28.43pt) yet with a *larger* total extension (89.54pt vs. ~60.4pt). This
weakens any assumption that the mechanism is direction-specific (i.e., tied to a downside
liquidity/panic dynamic) — the same "large gap, fails to retrace, extends" behavior appears
available to both directions. It is filed as a single instance, not a confirmed symmetric rule.
