H1 volumes for the full session so far (2025-08-07, XAUUSD):

|---|---|
| 00:00-01:00 | 30,168 |

After the original 00:00-01:00 spike documented in v1, volume did not simply revert to the prior
baseline (roughly 4,500-6,400/hour on 08-04 through 08-06) — hours 02:00-05:00 settled at
11,775-14,883, still 2-3x the established quiet-hour baseline, before a second spike (05:00-06:00,
32,049) matching the original hour's scale. On M15/M1, the 05:30 UTC leg (7.4pt decline) showed the
same distributed, multi-minute participation as the 00:00-01:00 move, with no single-candle
concentration and no unusual absorption or reaction signature — an unremarkable instance of the
already-established construction type.

## Why This Matters To DC-0010

This complicates the original framing. v1 described the 00:00-01:00 hour specifically as the one
that broke from an established quiet-hour pattern. The fuller picture is that the entire session
from 00:00 UTC onward has run persistently hotter than the 08-04/08-05/08-06 baseline, with two
distinct spikes (00:00-01:00 and 05:00-06:00) separated by an elevated-but-lesser plateau, rather
than one isolated anomalous hour surrounded by a return to normal. Whether this is a whole-session
phenomenon (something about 2025-08-07 specifically, day-of-week or otherwise) rather than an
hour-specific one is the open question this addendum raises — it does not resolve it.
