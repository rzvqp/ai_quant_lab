## New Observation

On 2026-01-28, around 15:00 UTC (NY-morning open), following a record-setting overnight rally
(peak 5311.665 — see Addendum A to DC-0024), price reversed sharply over **two consecutive M15
candles**:

| Candle (UTC) | O-H-L-C | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| 15:00-15:15 | 5285.925 / 5298.35 / 5235.75 / 5256.535 | **42,339** |
| 15:15-15:30 | 5256.54 / 5274.645 / 5244.76 / 5269.545 | **42,808** |

**Both candles exceed this candidate's own Addendum A record (41,995)** — the first by 344 (+0.8%),
the second by 813 (+1.9%), making the second candle the **new all-time #1 single-candle volume
record for this replay**, and the first time two consecutive candles have both stood above the
prior all-time high.

Dropping to M5: the first candle (42,339) splits 9,268/15,000/18,071 across its three sub-candles —
largest share 18,071/42,339 = **42.68%**, marginally under the 42.7% reference (a hair's-width
margin, flagged honestly rather than treated as decisively organic). Within that same sub-candle
(15:05-15:10 UTC), price fell from ~5293.9 to an intrabar low of 5235.75 — **~58 points in 5
minutes**, the fastest point-per-minute velocity documented anywhere in this family so far, faster
than both the base candidate (83.9pt/30min) and Addendum A (99.09pt/45min). The second candle
(42,808) splits 16,226/13,106/13,476 — largest share 16,226/42,808 = **37.9%**, clearly organic.

Total reversal magnitude: from the pre-reversal high (5298.35) to the low (5235.75) = **75.915
points in 30 minutes**. After the low, price recovered partially (to 5269.545 by the end of the
observed window, ~33.8pt / ~44.5% of the decline) and the resolution was still in progress as of
this filing.

## Why This Matters To DC-0025

This sets a **new all-time #1 volume record**, and for the first time shows **two consecutive**
candles both above the previous all-time high — a denser concentration of extreme volume than any
prior instance in this family (the base candidate and Addendum A each had one standout record
candle; this instance has two in a row). The internal ~58pt/5-minute sub-move is also the fastest
point-velocity yet documented for this mechanism. Notably, this instance is a **reversal out of a
record-setting rally** rather than a decline from ordinary consolidation (the base candidate and
Addendum A both began from quiet/consolidating conditions) — raising an open question about whether
this family's most extreme volume events cluster at turning points following large prior moves, not
addressed by this single instance.
