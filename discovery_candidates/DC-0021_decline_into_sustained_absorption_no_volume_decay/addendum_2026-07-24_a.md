# Addendum A to DC-0021 (2026-07-24)

## New evidence

A second instance of the same two-phase mechanism (sustained-volume decline handing off directly,
with no volume decay at the transition, into a sustained-volume absorption phase) occurred the very
next trading day, again within the NY-morning/NY-open window:

| Candle (UTC) | O-H-L-C | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| 14:30-14:45 | 4000.535 / 4000.535 / **3984.385** / 3990.575 | **19,232** |
| 14:45-15:00 | 3990.665 / 3995.875 / 3986.045 / 3993.605 | 13,912 |
| 15:00-15:15 | 3993.56 / 3999.285 / 3990.74 / 3991.305 | 14,372 |
| 15:15-15:30 | 3991.30 / 3995.125 / 3987.915 / 3993.385 | 15,642 |
| 15:30-15:45 | 3993.355 / 3995.82 / 3985.985 / 3986.55 | 13,290 |
| 15:45-16:00 | 3986.51 / 3992.955 / 3984.01 / 3991.05 | 8,725 |
| 16:00-16:15 | 3991.095 / 4000.915 / 3990.165 / 3999.565 | 10,133 |

**Phase 1 (14:30-14:45 UTC, 1 candle)**: a sharp decline of ~16.15 points (4000.535 -> 3984.385) on
the episode's peak volume, 19,232 — in this instance the peak volume arrives immediately in a
single candle rather than ramping up gradually over several candles as in the original DC-0021
observation.

**Phase 2 (14:45-15:45 UTC, 4 candles, ~1h)**: price oscillates within a ~3985-3999 band (minimal
net displacement) while volume remains elevated (13,290-15,642) for all four candles — again, no
proportional decay at the phase 1 -> phase 2 boundary.

**Resolution (15:45-16:15 UTC, 2 candles)**: volume finally decays (8,725, then 10,133) and price
resolves with a bullish breakout to a fresh local high (4000.915), above the pre-decline level —
the opposite resolution direction from the original DC-0021 instance, which ended in continued mild
decline/consolidation below its pre-decline level.

Dropping to M5 on the peak-volume candle (14:30-14:45 UTC, 19,232), volume splits 7,041 / 6,927 /
5,264 across the three sub-candles — largest share 7,041/19,232 = 36.6%, below the 42.7%
concentration ratio, confirming organic construction.

## Why this matters

This confirms the core two-phase, no-volume-decay-at-the-boundary shape documented in DC-0021 is
not a one-off (n=2 now, both within the same NY-morning/NY-open session window, on consecutive
trading days). It also introduces two points of variation: (a) the decline phase can arrive as a
single sharp-volume spike rather than a gradual multi-candle ramp, and (b) the eventual resolution
direction (bullish breakout here vs. continued decline in the original instance) is not fixed by
the mechanism itself — the absorption phase's outcome does not obviously telegraph its own
direction from volume alone.

## Confidence

Still **Low**. n=2, both instances drawn from the same local sample and the same session window
(NY-morning/NY-open), on consecutive calendar days — this may reflect a session-timing regularity
in this narrow window rather than a general market mechanism. No claim is made about resolution
direction being predictable from the phase-2 volume/price behavior observed so far.
