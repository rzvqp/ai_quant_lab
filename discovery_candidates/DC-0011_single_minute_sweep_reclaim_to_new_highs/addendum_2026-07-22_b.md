## New Observation: Same Outcome, Reclaim Distributed Across Several Minutes Instead Of One

On 2025-08-11, after a roughly 2h15m quiet consolidation (approx. 15:15-17:30 UTC, range
confined to about 3341-3350.4), the M15 candle 17:30-17:45 UTC carried O3345.405 H3358.65
**L3341.155** C3358.45 — volume 20,724, a sharp step up from the preceding several hours'
5,000-9,000 range.

Dropping to M5 then M1: the low (3341.155) was made in the 17:30-17:35 M5 candle, briefly
piercing the consolidation floor. The reclaim, however, did not resemble the single-minute spike
in v1 or in Addendum A. Instead, price climbed in a comparatively steady sequence across the next
five one-minute candles (17:35-17:39 UTC): 3343.3->3348.8->3350.6->3352.4->3354.1->3355.3, each
carrying its own material (declining but non-trivial) volume — 2439, 2636, 2631, 1928, 1492 — rather
than one dominant-volume minute followed by quiet drift. Price then continued, on lighter volume,
to 3358.65 by 17:45 UTC, exceeding the pre-sweep consolidation ceiling (~3350.4).

## Why This Matters To DC-0011

The outcome matches v1's pattern exactly — a low is swept and price not only reclaims but extends
to new highs beyond the pre-sweep range. But the construction of the reclaim itself looks different:
here it resembles a sustained, multi-minute build (compatible with the contrast already drawn in
DC-0008 between single-minute-concentrated and multi-minute-sustained construction) rather than a
single dominant-volume reversal minute as in v1 and Addendum A. Whether "sweep + reclaim" as a
category has two distinct reclaim-construction subtypes (concentrated vs. sustained), or whether
this is just sample variation, is not established by three instances.
