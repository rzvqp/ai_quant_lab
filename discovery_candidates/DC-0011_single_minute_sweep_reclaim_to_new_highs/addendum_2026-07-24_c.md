## New Observation: The Same "Sweep Reclaimed, Extends To New Highs" Shape At A Much Larger Scale, With Near-Record Volume

On 2025-12-10, NY-afternoon session, following an ordinary quiet baseline (~4193-4197, volume
1.2k-2.5k per M15 candle), a sudden multi-candle sweep/decline phase began at 19:00 UTC: two
down-legs over roughly 45 minutes, the second reaching a low of 4182.08 (touched within the
19:30-19:45 UTC M15 candle). That candle carried volume 34,319 — the **third-highest single-candle
volume in the entire replay**, behind only DC-0020's all-time record (37,204) and DC-0018's
36,798.

Dropping to M5 to check organic construction: the peak-volume candle splits 9,207 / 11,885 / 13,227
— largest share 13,227/34,319 = 38.5%, below the 42.7% concentration reference, confirming organic
construction (not a data artifact). The episode's opening candle (19:00-19:15 UTC, volume 24,233,
itself the fourth-highest of the replay) was checked the same way: 8,952 / 8,001 / 7,280 — largest
share 36.9%, also organic.

After the 4182.08 low, price reversed sharply and did not merely reclaim the pre-episode level
(~4193-4197) — it extended well past it, to a fresh high of 4238.785 by 20:15-20:30 UTC, a full
round trip of ~56.7 points from the episode low. Volume decayed steadily as the rally extended
(from the 34,319 peak down to 4,900-6,200 per candle by the time the new high was set).

## Why This Matters To DC-0011

This is the exact mechanism v1 documents — a sweep/decline is reclaimed and the subsequent move
extends past the pre-sweep range rather than merely stabilizing back at it — repeating at a much
larger scale: multiple M15 candles and ~1h30m instead of a single minute, and near-record volume
(34,319, third-highest ever) instead of a 1.5-3x local multiple. The directional outcome (reclaim
-> new high, not reclaim -> stall) is identical to v1's finding.

Three-part novelty test applied explicitly (CEO directive) before deciding artifact type: (1) Is
this a new MECHANISM? No — it is DC-0011's mechanism (sweep reclaimed, extends past pre-sweep
range to a new high), observed again. (2) Could this be filed only as an Addendum? Yes — that is
exactly what this document is; the shape match to DC-0011 is close enough that this is squarely
further evidence for that candidate, not a new claim. (3) Is this a new record? No — 34,319 ranks
third all-time (below DC-0020's 37,204 and DC-0018's 36,798), and the ~56.7pt round-trip magnitude
is below the all-time records (100pt+). It is, however, notably close to the volume records and is
recorded here for that reason.
