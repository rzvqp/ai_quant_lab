## New Observation

On 2025-10-21, during London mid-morning (07:30-08:30 UTC — distinct from the early-London
05:30-06:45 UTC window already documented in Addendum D), price declined from a high of 4334.57 to
a low of 4243.76 — a **90.81-point move, the third-largest displacement observed so far in this
family**, behind Addendum F's 100.97pt and Addendum E's 93.15pt. The decline unfolded over four M15
candles on a volume band of 9997, 9287, 11451 — consistent with the family's now-established
9,000-13,000 range (extended upward by Addendum F). Dropping to M5 confirmed the same distributed,
non-concentrated construction: volume per 5-minute bar ranged 1827-3922 with no single dominant bar,
rising during the steepest segment of the decline (3734, 3922, 3795 across the three 5-minute bars
spanning the final, sharpest leg from 4298.28 to 4243.76) — ruling out a data-artifact signature
(contrast with `DATA_QUALITY_OPEN_ITEM_2025-09-17_1800UTC.md`).

Following the low, price recovered 30.35 points to 4274.11 over the next five M15 candles on
declining volume (10872, 9267, 7145, and below), then stabilized in a 4267-4274 band. This is a
**partial recovery of roughly one-third the decline's magnitude** — the same resolution flavor
first documented in Addendum F (partial bounce followed by consolidation at an intermediate level),
now observed a second time, rather than the clean full recovery of Addendum E or the simple
consolidation of the original/A/B/D.

This is the **fifth distinct session/hour** in which this construction has now been documented: New
York midday (original, A, B, C), early London (D, 05:30-06:45 UTC), early Asia (E, 00:45-01:30
UTC), NY pre-open/early NY (F, 13:30-14:15 UTC), and now London mid-morning (07:30-08:30 UTC).

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This addendum adds a second data point to the "partial recovery" resolution type first seen in
Addendum F, at a comparable magnitude (90.81pt vs. F's 100.97pt) and comparable retracement fraction
(roughly one-third in both cases) but in a different session. This modestly firms up — without yet
establishing — the tentative pattern floated in Addendum F: that instances of this construction
exceeding roughly 90 points may tend toward partial-recovery resolutions rather than the clean
consolidation or clean full-recovery styles seen in smaller instances. It also further extends the
session-independence finding from Addendum D/E/F: this construction has now been documented in five
different session/hour contexts, with no clear pattern yet linking session to magnitude, volume, or
resolution style.

This remains n=2 for the specific "large-magnitude, partial-recovery" combination and should not be
read as an established rule — smaller-magnitude instances in other sessions have continued to show
simple consolidation (this same session/hour window, 07:15-09:00 UTC on 2025-10-17, showed a much
smaller ~45.75pt decline that consolidated normally, per the SESSION_STATE.md journal), so magnitude
rather than session/hour appears to be the more relevant variable for resolution style — itself only
a hedge-worthy observation from n=2.
