## New Observation

On 2025-10-28, starting around 05:30 UTC — the same early-London pre-open window documented in
Addendum D — price declined from a local high of 3980.1 to an intrabar low of 3886.465, a move of
**approximately 89-94pt depending on reference point** (93.6pt high-to-low intrabar, ~85pt
close-to-close), essentially **tying Addendum D's own record for this family (89.37pt)**, the
largest single directional displacement previously documented. Where this instance differs sharply
from Addendum D is construction: instead of D's contained 5-6 candle, ~1h15m decline, this move
unfolded over roughly **14 M15 candles (~3h30m, 05:30-09:00 UTC)** — more than double the duration
for essentially the same total magnitude. Per-candle volume ranged 6,812-14,011 (two candles briefly
above 12k; the majority clustered 8k-11k) — a similar but somewhat wider and, on average, lower band
than Addendum D's tight 9,863-11,816. Dropping to M5 confirmed evenly distributed, organic
construction throughout (roughly 1,900-5,050 per 5-minute sub-candle, no dominant sub-candle, no
concentration) — not a data artifact.

The decline also crossed directly through the window already reverified multiple times without
repeat (05:30-06:45 UTC, the Addendum D window itself) and continued into the window associated with
Addendum G (07:30 UTC onward, a different session/day) as one continuous move, rather than the two
being separate events — the first time these two previously-distinct clock windows have been
observed as a single uninterrupted decline. The move resolved via consolidation/partial recovery
(two candles back to ~3910, matching the family's usual resolution style) rather than a sharp
reversal or further extension.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This shows the same near-maximal magnitude for this construction (~89-94pt, tying/marginally
exceeding Addendum D) can be reached via two structurally different paths: a contained, ~1h15m,
tightly-banded-volume burst (Addendum D), or a slower, ~3h30m grind at a wider and generally lower
volume band (this instance) that happens to span two previously-separate flagged clock windows as
one continuous move. This weakens any assumption that this family's total displacement is tied to a
particular pace or volume intensity — the same endpoint magnitude appears reachable at very
different speeds. It also raises a question not previously visible: are the 05:30-06:45 and
07:30-09:00 UTC windows (previously tracked as separate, both individually reverified many times
without repeating their respective addenda) actually two segments of one longer early-session
decline mechanism that happens to pause partway through, rather than two independent phenomena? This
rests on a single instance and is filed as a question, not a conclusion.
