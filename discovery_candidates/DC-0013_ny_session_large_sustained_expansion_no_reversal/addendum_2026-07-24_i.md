## New Observation

On 2025-10-22, at exactly the 00:00 UTC hour boundary, price declined from 4124.60 (23:45 UTC,
2025-10-21) to a low of 4004.54 (00:00-00:15 UTC) — a **120.06-point move, the second-largest
displacement observed in this family** after Addendum H's 180.53pt. Unlike every other large
instance, this decline was compressed into just **two M15 candles (30 minutes)** rather than the
usual three-to-twelve, making it the fastest large-magnitude instance yet relative to its size.
Volume across the move was 6846 (the lead-in candle, sub-threshold), then 11726 and 10659 for the
two decline candles — within the family's now-established 9,000-13,000 band. Dropping to M5
confirmed the same distributed, non-concentrated construction: volume per 5-minute bar rose steadily
through the decline (1515, 2406, 2925, 3495, 4138, 4093) with no single dominant bar, tracking the
accelerating price action rather than sitting flat — ruling out a data-artifact signature (contrast
with `DATA_QUALITY_OPEN_ITEM_2025-09-17_1800UTC.md`).

Following the low, price recovered 116.27 points over the next six M15 candles (00:15-01:30 UTC) to
a high of 4120.81 — within half a point of the original 4124.60 pre-decline level, a **roughly 97%
retracement**, the most complete recovery documented in this family (exceeding Addendum E's ~71%
retracement of a smaller decline). Volume during the recovery declined steadily from 10659 to
8013-8123 as the move matured, then price stabilized in the 4098-4110 area.

This instance occurs at precisely the 00:00 UTC hour already covered by `DC-0014` (a V-shaped
reversal building into a sustained rally, then reversing, first observed 2025-08-26) and partially
overlapping the early-Asia window of Addendum E (00:45-01:30 UTC). Unlike DC-0014's much smaller
(~35.4pt) rally-then-reversal sequence, this instance is a decline-then-near-full-recovery at more
than 3x the magnitude, compressed into a much shorter initial move.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This is the second data point (after Addendum E) showing this construction resolve via recovery
rather than consolidation or partial bounce, and it sets a new record for both magnitude (120.06pt,
second only to Addendum H) and completeness of retracement (~97%, vs Addendum E's ~71%) within the
"recovery" resolution sub-type. It also demonstrates that a large-magnitude instance of this
construction can compress its entire directional move into as few as two candles — previously, the
family's largest instances (Addendum E, F, G, H) all took at least three candles and often many
more to reach their extremes. Combined with Addendum H's finding that very large instances can also
stretch to many hours with internal oscillation, this addendum shows the opposite extreme is also
possible: a large, fast, clean move resolved efficiently. Taken together, Addenda E through I now
show this construction's resolution and pacing varies considerably at large magnitudes, with no
single dominant style — consolidation, partial bounce, full recovery, and multi-hour internal
oscillation have all now been observed, each only once or twice (n=1-2 per style), and this remains
too thin an evidence base to characterize which style is typical.
