## New Observation

On 2025-10-17, during the NY pre-open / early-NY window, price declined from a high of 4317.91
(13:15-13:30 UTC) to a low of 4216.94 (within the 14:00-14:15 UTC candle) — a **100.97-point move,
the largest single directional displacement observed so far in this family**, exceeding Addendum
E's previous record (~93.15pt). The decline unfolded over three M15 candles (13:30-14:15 UTC) on a
volume band of 12533, 11930, 12768 — for the first time in this family, this is **consistently
above** the 9,000-12,000 band documented in Addendum B/C/D/E, extending the family's known volume
range upward by roughly 1,000-2,800 units. Dropping to M5 confirmed the same distributed,
non-concentrated construction already established for this family: volume per 5-minute sub-candle
was remarkably tight (3,934-4,373) with no dominant sub-candle across all nine 5-minute bars
spanning the decline, and it rose modestly during the two steepest wick segments — ruling out a
data-artifact signature (contrast with `DATA_QUALITY_OPEN_ITEM_2025-09-17_1800UTC.md`).

Following the low, price bounced 49.44 points to a local high of 4266.38 (14:30-14:45 UTC) on
continued elevated volume (12293, 11909), then — unlike Addendum E's clean four-candle sustained
recovery — spent the next four M15 candles chopping within a 4231-4260 range (volumes 11281, 10718,
10708, gradually declining) before finally stabilizing as volume dropped to 8549 and below. The net
round-trip (13:30-16:00 UTC, ~2h30m) left price roughly 62-70 points below the original peak,
neither fully retracing (like Addendum E) nor cleanly consolidating near the low (like the
original/Addendum A/B/D).

This is also the **fourth distinct session** in which this moderate-to-elevated-volume, multi-candle
construction has now been documented: New York midday (DC-0013 original, Addendum A, B, C), early
London (Addendum D, 05:30-06:45 UTC), early Asia (Addendum E, 00:45-01:30 UTC), and now NY
pre-open/early NY (13:30-14:15 UTC).

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This addendum extends three findings simultaneously. First, it sets a new magnitude record
(100.97pt), now comfortably past the psychologically round 100-point mark. Second, it is the first
instance whose volume consistently exceeds the previously tight 9,000-12,000 band, suggesting that
band was itself provisional rather than a hard ceiling — the construction appears to scale somewhat
with magnitude rather than being capped at a fixed volume level. Third, its resolution is a third
distinct flavor: neither the clean consolidation of the original/A/B/D, nor the clean full recovery
of Addendum E, but a partial (~49%) bounce followed by extended choppy consolidation at an
intermediate level. Taken together with Addendum E, this suggests that once a decline of this
construction exceeds roughly 90 points, the "what happens next" question resolves less
predictably than the smaller instances — with genuine partial-recovery and choppy-consolidation
outcomes both now observed, rather than a single dominant resolution style. This is explicitly
noted as description, not prediction: with n=1 for each of the two large-magnitude instances
(Addendum E and this one), no claim is made about which resolution style is more likely to recur.
