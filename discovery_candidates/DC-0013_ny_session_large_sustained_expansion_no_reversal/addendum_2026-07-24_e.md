## New Observation

On 2025-10-17, during the early Asia session, price declined from a high of 4371.58 (00:45 UTC) to
a low of 4278.43 (01:15-01:30 UTC) — a **93.15-point move, the largest single directional
displacement observed so far in this family**, exceeding Addendum D's previous record (~89.4pt).
The decline unfolded over three M15 candles (00:45-01:30 UTC) on a tight, consistent volume band
of 10317, 9508, 10609 — closely matching the family's documented 9,000-12,000 moderate-volume
signature. Dropping to M5 confirmed the same distributed, non-concentrated construction already
established for this family: volume per 5-minute sub-candle ranged narrowly (2072-3884) with no
single dominant sub-candle, rising as the decline accelerated into its steepest segment — ruling
out a data-artifact signature (contrast with `DATA_QUALITY_OPEN_ITEM_2025-09-17_1800UTC.md`).

Unlike every prior instance in this family (DC-0013 original, Addendum A, B, and D — all of which
resolved via consolidation/stabilization near the post-decline level), this instance resolved
differently: the four M15 candles following the low (01:30-02:30 UTC) built a **sustained recovery
of comparable magnitude** — from 4278.43 back up to 4344.64, a 66.21-point rally — on the same
tight moderate-volume band (10593, 10230, 8140, 7632, the last two already declining out of the
band as the move matured). The net round-trip (00:45-02:30 UTC, ~1h45m) left price roughly 27-31pt
below the original peak rather than fully retracing, before volume dropped below 7,000 and price
stabilized in a 4336-4348 band.

This is also the **third distinct session/hour** in which this moderate-volume, multi-candle,
sustained construction has now been documented: New York (DC-0013 original, Addendum A, B, C),
early London (Addendum D, 05:30-06:45 UTC), and now early Asia (00:45-01:30 UTC for the decline
leg, extending to 02:30 UTC for the recovery leg).

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This addendum extends two findings simultaneously. First, it sets a new magnitude record (93.15pt)
while remaining within the same 9,000-12,000 volume band already established in Addendum B/C/D,
further weakening any "high volume required" or "session-specific" characterization of this
construction. Second, and more significantly, it introduces a **genuinely new resolution type**:
every prior instance ended in consolidation/stabilization near the decline's endpoint; this instance
instead built a second, comparably large, sustained-volume leg in the opposite direction before
finally stabilizing. Whether this is a distinct sub-type (a "round-trip" variant) or simply the
tail of a continuous distribution of resolution outcomes cannot be established from a single
instance (n=1 for this specific round-trip shape).

This instance also sits within the same broad 00:00-02:00 UTC clock window already covered by
`DC-0014` (a V-shaped reversal building into a sustained four-candle rally, then reversing, first
observed 2025-08-26). The two candidates share a superficial family resemblance (multi-candle,
sustained-volume, direction-reversing construction at this general hour) but differ in sequence and
scale: DC-0014 begins with a V-reversal from a fresh low into a *rally* that later reverses down
(~35.4pt total), while this instance begins from an ordinary level, builds a large *decline*
first (93.15pt), and only then recovers. Given the difference in sequencing and scale, this
addendum is filed against DC-0013 (the moderate-volume decline construction it most directly
extends) rather than against DC-0014, but the overlap is noted here as a cross-reference worth
revisiting if a future instance blurs the distinction further.
