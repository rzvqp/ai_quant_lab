## New Observation

On 2025-10-21, spanning London late-morning through NY afternoon (11:45-18:15 UTC), price declined
from a high of 4261.07 to a low of 4080.54 — a **180.53-point move, by far the largest
displacement observed in this family**, more than 1.5x Addendum F's previous record of 100.97pt.
The decline itself unfolded over roughly twelve M15 candles (11:45-14:45 UTC, 3 hours) on volume
that stayed in a 9,369-12,743 band throughout — squarely within the range already established by
Addendum F, but sustained for far longer than any prior instance (previous longest was 8 candles).
Dropping to M5 for the sharpest segment of the decline (16:00-16:45 UTC region containing the
final plunge to the 4080.54 low) confirmed the same distributed, non-concentrated construction:
volume per 5-minute bar ranged 3,773-4,348 with no single dominant bar, rising modestly during the
fastest portion of the decline — consistent with every prior instance in this family and ruling out
a data-artifact signature (contrast with `DATA_QUALITY_OPEN_ITEM_2025-09-17_1800UTC.md`).

Following the low, price did not cleanly recover, cleanly consolidate, or cleanly partial-bounce as
in prior addenda. Instead it spent roughly **3.5 further hours (14:45-18:15 UTC, ~14 more M15
candles)** oscillating within a wide 4080-4267 range — several distinct legs of partial recovery and
renewed decline, each individually resembling smaller instances of this same construction — before
volume finally dropped below 8,000 and price stabilized around 4111-4119. Volume remained elevated
(mostly 8,000-12,700) across nearly this entire multi-hour span, making this a single continuous
episode of sustained participation lasting roughly **6.5 hours total (11:45-18:15 UTC) across
roughly 26 M15 candles** — more than triple the duration of any prior instance in this family.

This episode's timespan overlaps three previously-documented windows for this and related
constructions: the 12:30 UTC hour (DC-0017 family), the NY morning open, and the 15:00-16:30 UTC
clock-clustering window flagged in Addendum C — none of which, examined individually, would have
predicted an episode of this scale.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

Every prior addendum (B through G) documented single, discrete instances lasting a few candles to
roughly two hours, each resolving into one of three styles: simple consolidation, full recovery, or
partial bounce. This instance breaks that pattern structurally, not just in magnitude: rather than
one clean leg followed by one resolution, it shows **multiple internal legs of decline and partial
recovery, all sustained on the family's characteristic volume band, strung together into one
extended episode**. This raises a question not previously posed by this family: whether the
"single clean move" framing implicit in addenda B-G is itself only accurate for smaller-magnitude
instances, and whether sufficiently large moves are better described as a sequence of smaller
moves from the same underlying mechanism rather than one atomic event. This candidate does not
resolve that question — it is filed as a single data point (n=1) at a scale roughly double anything
seen before, and the observation is offered with an explicit hedge: one very large, very long
episode is not evidence that such episodes are common, nor that they will resolve the same way if
they recur.
