## New Observation

On 2025-10-14, during **early London pre-open hours** (05:30-06:45 UTC — NOT the New York session
that every prior instance of this family occupied), price declined from a high of 4179.78 to a low
of 4090.41 — an **89.37-point move, the largest single directional displacement observed so far in
this family**, exceeding Addendum B's previous record (~71.58pt), DC-0013's original (~43pt),
Addendum A's (~32-38pt), DC-0016's (~47.2pt), and DC-0018's (~47.8pt). The decline unfolded over
five to six M15 candles (~1h15m) and, unlike the cleaner near-monotonic declines of DC-0013's
original/Addendum A/Addendum B, contained several internal counter-swings (e.g., a bounce from
4119.1 to 4137.51, then back down to 4110.9, another bounce to 4134.25, then down to 4090.41) —
more two-sided/choppy in construction than prior instances, though the net displacement and
overall directional bias remained clearly downward throughout. The move resolved the same way as
DC-0013's original, Addendum A, and Addendum B: consolidation/stabilization in a 4098-4113 band
over the following two candles (volume declining to 8909, then 7757) rather than a sharp V-reversal
or continuation to new extremes.

Per-candle M15 volume across the five decline candles was 11506, 11816, 10896, 11232, 9863 — a
tight, consistent band closely matching Addendum B/C's documented 9,000-12,000 moderate-volume
signature (well below DC-0013's original 15,000-30,000+ range). Dropping to M5 confirmed the same
distributed, non-concentrated construction already established for this family: volume per
5-minute sub-candle ranged narrowly (1842-4284) with no single dominant sub-candle, and it rose
during the steepest part of the decline (05:45-06:00 UTC, the fastest ~30pt segment) rather than
sitting flat/uniform — ruling out a data-artifact signature (contrast with
`DATA_QUALITY_OPEN_ITEM_2025-09-17_1800UTC.md`).

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

Every prior instance of this construction (DC-0013 original, Addendum A, Addendum B, and the
Addendum C clock-clustering instances) occurred during the New York session, specifically within
roughly 15:00-19:45 UTC. This instance breaks that pattern entirely: it is the **first known
occurrence of this moderate-volume, multi-candle, no-sharp-reversal construction outside the NY
session** — here during early London pre-open hours. This weakens any working assumption that the
construction is a NY-session-specific phenomenon (e.g., tied to NY liquidity/flow patterns) and
raises the alternative possibility that it reflects a more general mechanism not tied to a specific
trading session. Combined with the fact that this is also the largest-magnitude instance yet
recorded (exceeding Addendum B by ~18pt) while remaining within the same moderate-volume band, this
addendum provides evidence that magnitude, volume-band, and resolution-style are durable features
of this construction independent of session — while session/time-of-day is not. The added internal
choppiness (counter-swings within the net decline) is noted as a point of difference from the
cleanest prior instances and is not yet understood to be meaningful on its own (n=1 for this
specific combination of session + choppiness); it is filed here for future comparison, not as an
established sub-type.

This is a single instance (n=1 outside the NY session) and does not establish that the construction
occurs with any particular frequency or regularity outside NY hours — it only establishes that it
*can* occur outside NY hours, which was previously unknown.
