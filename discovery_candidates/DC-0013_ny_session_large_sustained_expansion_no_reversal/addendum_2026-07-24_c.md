# Addendum C to DC-0013 (dated 2026-07-24)

## New Observation

Addendum B documented that this family's "large magnitude at moderate volume" instances share a
consistent per-candle volume range (roughly 9,000-12,000). This addendum adds a distinct new
dimension: **clock-time recurrence**. Three instances of this moderate-volume construction have
now occurred within a short span, and two of them fall in the identical 15:00-16:30 UTC clock
window on consecutive calendar days:

- **2025-10-09, 15:15-16:30 UTC**: decline from 4056.78 to 4004.44 (~52.3pt) over 6 candles,
  volume 9,359-11,783 (documented in Addendum B).
- **2025-10-09, 18:15-19:45 UTC**: decline from 4012.53 to 3944.77 (~67.8pt) over 6 candles,
  volume 9,282-11,469 (same day, different clock hour — logged in session journal as a
  same-day confirming instance, no separate addendum).
- **2025-10-10, 15:00-15:45 UTC**: decline from 3998.11 to 3970.93 (~27.2pt) over 3 candles,
  volume 10,410-10,703 — smaller in magnitude than the prior two, but occurring in the *same*
  15:00-16:30 UTC window as the first 2025-10-09 instance, one calendar day later.

All three instances resolved the same way: a sustained decline on consistent volume (never
dropping below ~9,300 or exceeding ~11,800 across all three), followed by stabilization/partial
recovery rather than a sharp V-reversal or a continuation to new extremes.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

Addendum B established the volume signature (9-12k, well below the family's original 15-30k+
threshold) as a genuine variant of this construction, weakening the "high volume required"
characterization. This addendum adds a second, distinct observation: two of the three known
low-to-moderate-volume instances fall in the *same specific clock-time window* (15:00-16:30 UTC)
on consecutive trading days. This raises — without yet establishing — the possibility that this
particular hour carries a structural tendency toward this construction (e.g., a recurring
liquidity/flow pattern specific to that time of day), rather than the moderate-volume declines
being purely randomly distributed across the NY session. The evidence is far too thin to draw
this conclusion with any confidence (n=2 for the specific clock window, n=3 for the volume
signature overall, across only two consecutive calendar days) — it is flagged here strictly as a
pattern worth comparing against in future observation, not as an established regularity.
