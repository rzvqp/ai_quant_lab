## New Observation

On 2025-10-27 (Monday), starting at the 13:30-13:45 UTC candle, price declined from a pre-decline
close of 4033.95 to an intrabar low of 3971.445 (15:00-15:15 UTC) — approximately **62.5pt intrabar
/ 55pt close-to-close** — across **7 consecutive M15 candles (13:30-15:15 UTC, ~1h45m)**, in the
NY pre-open/early-NY window already documented for this family (same general session as Addendum F,
2025-10-17 13:30-16:00 UTC).

What is new is the volume: each of the 7 candles carried **19.5k-26.9k volume**, with six of the
seven at or above 21.7k. This is by a wide margin the highest **sustained** volume band recorded for
this family — nearly double Addendum F's own record (11.9k-12.8k, itself flagged at the time as
"first instance consistently over the 9-12k band"), and approaching (without reaching) DC-0017's
NFP-day volume (30,975). Dropping to M5 confirmed the same distributed, organic construction already
catalogued across this family: 5-minute sub-bars ran roughly 6k-10k with no single M5 candle
exceeding about 37% of its parent M15 total.

Following the low, four candles of recovery/stabilization (15:15-16:15 UTC, volume fading from
19.5k down to 12.4k-15k) recovered price to 3993.87 — roughly 15 of the ~62.5pt decline, a partial,
not full or near-full, recovery so far.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This is the second time in this family's history that a new volume-band record has been the
qualifying new dimension rather than magnitude or duration (the first was Addendum F). Here,
magnitude (~55-62.5pt) and duration (7 candles/~1h45m) both sit comfortably within ranges already
documented elsewhere in the family (well below the largest instances, e.g. Addendum H's ~180.53pt/
~6.5h) — it is the volume intensity for a given magnitude/duration that is unprecedented. Combined
with Addendum F, this weakens any assumption that the family's volume band is bounded near 9-12k;
two instances now, in the same general session, have each set a new sustained-volume record. Whether
this session (NY pre-open/early-NY) specifically tends toward higher-volume constructions than other
sessions in this family cannot be assessed from n=2, but is worth tracking.
