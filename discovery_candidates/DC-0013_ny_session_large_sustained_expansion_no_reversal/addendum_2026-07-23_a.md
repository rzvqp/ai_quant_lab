## New Observation

On 2025-09-02, starting near the NY session open (13:30 UTC), price advanced across seven
consecutive M15 candles (13:30-15:15 UTC) from approximately 3485.4 (open of the first candle,
low 3479 within it) to a peak high of 3516.91, roughly 31.5-37.9 points, before consolidating over
the following two candles (volume dropping from 15297 to 9276 then 10694, range confining to
roughly 3510-3516) rather than reversing sharply.

Unlike DC-0013's original instance -- which began with one exceptionally large single candle
(29,674 vol, 27.13pt) and then continued -- this instance built more gradually: per-candle volume
across the seven candles was 19156, 20005, 23646, 17449, 17031, 16233, 15297, with no single
candle standing out as dramatically as DC-0013's opening candle, but persisting elevated for
longer (seven candles vs four). Dropping to M5 on the highest-volume M15 candle (23646) showed
distribution across the three M5 sub-candles (7697/7631/8318, none exceeding ~35% of the total) --
the same sustained, distributed construction as DC-0008/DC-0013 and the DC-0016 family.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

DC-0013's Section 4 asked whether the "extends across multiple candles without reversal" behavior
recurs, or whether the original four-candle continuation was itself unusual. This instance answers
partially: the no-reversal, consolidation-not-reversal resolution DID recur (matching DC-0013
rather than the reversal-ending pattern seen in DC-0014/DC-0016), but the duration was longer
(seven candles instead of four) and the construction lacked DC-0013's single dramatic opening
candle, building more evenly instead. The overall magnitude (~32-38pt) is comparable to but somewhat
smaller than DC-0013's ~43pt. Taken together with DC-0015 (eleven candles, NY afternoon,
consolidation ending) and DC-0016/its Addendum A (reversal endings, shorter durations, different
session), this adds a data point suggesting NY-session-open expansions specifically may lean toward
a consolidation ending rather than a sharp reversal, though n=2 for this specific sub-window is far
too small to treat that as established.
