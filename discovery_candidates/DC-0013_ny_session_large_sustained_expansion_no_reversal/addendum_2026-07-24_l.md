## New Observation

On 2025-12-29, starting around 13:05 UTC (NY pre-open/early-NY), price declined from a local high
of 4467.7 to an intrabar low of 4302.11 — **165.59pt**, the **second-largest displacement in this
family** (behind only Addendum H's 180.53pt), over roughly **2h15m (13:05-15:20 UTC)**. Volume was
unusually concentrated at the high end even by this family's standards: **four consecutive M15
candles carried 34,453 / 29,809 / 28,227 / 28,172** — the peak candle (34,453) is the
**third-highest single-candle volume observed anywhere in this replay**, edging past the previous
third-place holder (34,319, DC-0011's Addendum C, 2025-12-10) by a narrow margin, and behind only
DC-0020's 37,204 and DC-0018's 36,798. Dropping to M5 to check organic construction on all four
peak candles: 34,453 splits 13,006/9,938/11,509 (largest share 37.75%); 29,809 splits
10,953/9,427/9,429 (36.7%); 28,227 splits 10,624/9,578/8,025 (37.6%); 28,172 splits 7,881/11,801/8,490
(41.9%, close to but still under the 42.7% reference) — all confirmed organic/distributed, not a
data artifact.

After the low, price recovered only partially (~32pt, to ~4330-4335) and consolidated there for
the remainder of the observed window, well below the pre-decline level — the family's usual
resolution style (partial recovery/consolidation, not a sharp V-reversal or further extension).

This investigation was initially logged in `OBSERVATION_REGISTRY.md` (entry 15) based on
observation through 14:20 UTC only, when the decline appeared to total ~81.3pt on volume 22k-27k —
unremarkable within the family. Continuing the walkthrough revealed the decline extended much
further (to 165.59pt) with volume escalating well past what was visible at the time entry 15 was
written. A follow-up note has been appended to that registry entry documenting the correction; this
addendum is filed separately because the fuller picture now shows a genuine near-record on two
axes (magnitude and volume) that the registry entry alone does not capture.

## Why This Matters To DC-0013

This is now the second-largest magnitude in the family (only Addendum H is larger), and its peak
candle sets a new (marginal) 3rd-place all-time volume rank for the entire replay — the first time
a DC-0013-family instance has approached the very top of the volume distribution rather than
staying within the family's previously-established bands (9-12k baseline, 19.5k-26.9k per Addendum
J). The four-consecutive-candle sustained 28k-34.5k volume plateau is also notable: it resembles
DC-0023/DC-0023-Addendum-A's "near-record volume floor sustained across several candles" character
more than it resembles any prior DC-0013 addendum, suggesting the upper end of this family's volume
range may overlap with the DC-0023 family's territory rather than being cleanly separate. This is
noted as an open question, not a conclusion, on a single instance.
