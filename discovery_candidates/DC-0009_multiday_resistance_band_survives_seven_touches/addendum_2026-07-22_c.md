## New Observation: Retest After The Break

Following the break documented in Addendum B (2025-08-04 11:00-11:45 UTC), price pulled back toward
the old band on the next three M15 candles:

- **12:00 UTC** — high 3371.595, then rejected, close 3367.06.
- **12:15 UTC** — continued down to close 3363.92, just above the old band ceiling (3363.6).
- **12:30 UTC** — low 3361.175 (dipped back inside the old band, which had held nine times as
  resistance), but closed 3363.22, at the boundary, on the highest volume of this whole window
  (5,483).
- **12:45 UTC** — reclaimed decisively, high 3369.01, close 3367.815, volume 4,958.

Price spent one candle (12:30) partly back inside the old resistance band before reclaiming above
it, rather than either holding cleanly above the old ceiling throughout, or fully re-entering and
consolidating there.

## Why This Matters To DC-0009

This completes an observable lifecycle for the same band across this window: resistance surviving
nine touches -> break with volume expansion -> a brief post-break dip back toward the broken level
-> reclaim. Whether the old ceiling acting briefly as support on this one retest generalizes to
other broken multi-touch levels is an open question this single instance cannot answer; it is
offered as one complete, countable example of the sequence.
