# RED TEAM — INDEPENDENCE RULES
### Standalone, enforceable law separating Red Team from Alpha
**Status:** ✅ v1.0 — APPROVED by CEO decision, 2026-07-21.
**Parent:** [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) §17.

> These rules exist so that the *incentive to find* (Alpha) and the *incentive to break* (Red Team) never share a head, a file tree, or a reward. A verdict produced without these guarantees is void.

---

## R1 — Personnel separation (recusal)
A reviewer who contributed, in any form, to a Discovery Candidate's discovery is **recused** from reviewing it. No one reviews their own work. Recusal is recorded in the ledger.

## R2 — Directional separation
Communication with Alpha is **one-way and artifact-mediated**: in = frozen submission package; out = verdict record. No live joint sessions on a candidate under review. A needed clarification that is not in the package is a **gap that counts against the candidate**, not a reason to open a dialogue.

## R3 — Repository separation
Red Team owns its own tree. It has **read-only** access to submitted packages and **no write access** to any Alpha or Flow C location. Alpha has **no write access** to Red Team's tree.

## R4 — Stance independence
Red Team treats every Discovery Candidate as an unverified scientific observation until sufficient evidence justifies further investigation — regardless of Alpha's confidence, reputation, or any prior 🟢 CONTINUE verdict on a different candidate — while still assuming Alpha submits in good faith and evaluating fairly (quality control, not destruction).

## R5 — Incentive independence
Red Team is **never** rewarded for candidates surviving or failing. It is rewarded only for the **correctness, reproducibility, and defensibility** of its verdicts. It must hold no stake in the direction of any outcome.

## R6 — Frozen-target rule
Red Team reviews only the frozen object bound by its hash. It refuses moving targets and mid-review edits; any change forces a **new DC id** and a new review.

## R7 — No-remedy rule
Red Team never advises how to make a candidate pass. It may state *why* a candidate did not survive (that is the verdict's content) and *that* further validation is required (with the question it must answer), but it never prescribes the cure. Prescribing the cure would turn Red Team into a second Alpha.

## R8 — Non-participation
Red Team reviewers do not participate in Alpha's discovery, and do not perform Flow C's interpretive analysis. Red Team performs no reproduction, validation, sensitivity, regime, or parameter work of its own (CEO refinement, 2026-07-21).

## R9 — Refusal right
If Red Team cannot conduct a review independently — pressure, incomplete freeze, forced collaboration, or a compromised package — it **declines and escalates to the CEO** rather than producing a compromised verdict.

## R10 — Attestation
Every review records a signed independence attestation confirming R1–R9 held for that review. No attestation → no valid verdict.
