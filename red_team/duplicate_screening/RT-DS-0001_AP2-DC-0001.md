## 4. ADVERSARIAL PASS ON WHAT IS NEW
*(DC-0018's own analysis is not repeated — see RED_TEAM_PHASE1_REPORT.md. Only the novel elements are attacked.)*

**A1 — Feed switch undermines the central evidence *(most serious; not raised by Alpha #2)*.** The metadata records that the feed label **alternates between `OANDA:XAUUSD` and `FUSIONMARKETS:XAUUSD` across this session**, verified as "not a chart-identity change." That verification is correct for *price* but does **not** license the volume comparison the candidate rests on. Volume on these feeds is **broker tick volume**, which is broker-specific: different client bases, different tick conventions, different absolute magnitudes. Every load-bearing claim here is a volume *ratio to a baseline* ("2.5–4× baseline", "~5 hours of continuously-elevated volume", "peak 12,627"). If the baseline was measured on one feed and the event on another — or if the label alternated mid-window — the elevation may be partly or wholly an artifact of the data source. **Until the volume series is confirmed to originate from a single feed across the whole 12:00–17:15 window, the candidate's principal evidence is unsafe.** This is the first thing to resolve.

**A2 — M15-only forecloses the decisive comparison.** Deliberate and honestly justified (replay data-loss hazard), but the consequence is structural: AP2 cannot say whether its breakout was sustained or single-minute-concentrated, so it cannot be reconciled with DC-0008 — the very distinction the catalog's Group I is built on.

**A3 — the "~40% larger second leg" is definitionally under-specified.** It is one measurement of a ratio, and the ratio moves substantially with arbitrary choices the document leaves open: the "pre-breakout origin" is given as a *range* (~2455–2465), and the decline can be measured to the extreme low (2411.59) or to the settle (2427–2433). Different defensible choices yield materially different ratios. A property that cannot be computed the same way twice is not yet a property.

**A4 — the calendar reading is already undercut by the catalog Alpha #2 could not see.** Alpha #2 correctly flags that it has not checked other first-Fridays. Red Team can: DC-0008's Addenda B/C/D document **five** 12:30 UTC instances with five different outcomes, Alpha #1 concluding "the only common thread being the clock time itself"; DC-0008-D shows both Fridays (NFP and non-NFP) sharing construction, reframing NFP→day-of-week; DC-0017-B shows a large *non-NFP* 12:30 print giving back its entire move. **AP2-DC-0001 becomes roughly the seventh catalogued 12:30 instance and the outcomes still do not converge.** The clock time does not determine the outcome. The calendar condition should therefore be treated as the *weakest*, not the headline, element of this submission.

**A5 — confidence calibration.** AP2 rates **Medium** at n=1 with no replication in its own history; Alpha #1 rates the comparable DC-0018 **Low** at n=1 *with* M1 anatomy. Under the portfolio's prevailing norm (n=1 → Low), Medium is generous, and it rests on event size rather than on evidential weight. *Red Team does not alter confidence — recorded as a calibration observation only.*

**A6 — no denominator.** "Does a breakout of this scale reliably fail?" is unanswerable without the count of comparable breakouts that did **not** fail. Portfolio-wide gap (Phase 1, F5) reproduced here.

**Execution is not Red Team's to perform.** Attaching the evidence would mean writing into Alpha #1's candidate folder, which the CEO forbids. Red Team therefore records the determination here and refers execution to the CEO.
