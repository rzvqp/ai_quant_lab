## §5 — Detached process & recovery · PASS (one non-blocking note)
PID `6232` is the single real decision interpreter (26880 is the git-bash `nohup &` wrapper, not a second decision instance) — survives session/launcher close. **Restart-dedup:** `LiveBarFeed` persists one watermark per `(symbol, timeframe)`; `poll()`'s dedup (`ts_open <= last_emitted`) is seeded from the **persisted** watermark, so a restart never re-emits already-processed bars. Circuit-breaker (`load_persisted_circuit_state`, not-READY → skip), authority, and gate are all re-read/reconstructed fresh on restart with fail-closed defaults; a stale-probe `BarFeedError` propagates and kills the process (fail-closed), never guesses. **Non-blocking recommendation:** there is no explicit singleton lock (pidfile/OS mutex) structurally preventing two concurrent decision loops. This is **not a safety defect** — two instances would both block at the frozen `enabled=False` gate (zero broker impact), and only one is running — but an explicit singleton guard is recommended hardening to avoid duplicate journaling under an accidental double-launch. A controlled restart (`taskkill //PID 6232 //T` → the SIGTERM handler finishes the in-flight tick and closes the store cleanly) requires no broker action and reconfirms the gate blocked before and after.
